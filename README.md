# Kydos Digital Agency Business-in-a-Box

This repository contains the complete operating blueprint for teaching a beginner how to start and run a UK digital marketing agency using the real Kydos Digital operating model as the worked example.

## Purpose

The programme is designed for people who may be starting with little or no digital marketing or agency experience. The goal is to help them establish a real UK agency, set up the right systems, recruit a delivery team, acquire clients, fulfil services, collect recurring revenue, manage finances and compliance, and scale towards an owner-led operating model.

This is not a white-label or reseller programme. Students build and own their own independent agency.

## Current Kydos operating principles

- UK digital marketing agency model
- First month can be used as a paid trial month
- Continuation moves to a 3, 6 or 12 month agreement
- Monthly services are paid in advance
- Account Managers own client strategy, copy, content planning, community management and client communication
- Creatives produce graphics, carousels and video edits
- One Account Manager normally handles 4 clients, with capacity up to 5
- One Creative normally handles 4 clients, with capacity up to 5
- Monthly content calendar due internally by the 25th of the previous month
- Operations Manager or CEO reviews the calendar before it is sent to the client
- Client approval window is 3 days
- Two revision rounds are included
- Weekly creative batch deadline is Monday at 1:00 PM UK time
- Reporting is monthly
- Meta Ads is Kydos's main acquisition channel
- Leads enter the CRM, receive automated email, SMS and WhatsApp follow-up, and the sales agent calls within 60 seconds
- Sales commission is 50% of the first service fee closed, excluding VAT
- Client information is stored in a dedicated Google Drive folder and collected through a Google Form
- No generic AI-generated visual designs are presented as standard professional creative work. Human creatives produce the designs and videos, unless the client specifically requests AI creative
