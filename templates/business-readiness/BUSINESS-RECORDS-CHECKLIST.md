# Business Records Checklist

This checklist is useful for ordinary business governance and can also support future due diligence, banking, accounting or sponsor-licence readiness.

## Companies House

- [ ] certificate of incorporation
- [ ] company number
- [ ] articles
- [ ] memorandum
- [ ] registered office
- [ ] registered email
- [ ] PSC records
- [ ] shareholder records
- [ ] director details
- [ ] confirmation statements
- [ ] annual accounts
- [ ] identity-verification records/personal codes stored securely

## Banking

- [ ] business bank account
- [ ] recent statements
- [ ] bank correspondence
- [ ] payment processor records
- [ ] Stripe settlements

## Accounting and tax

- [ ] FreeAgent/accounting records
- [ ] sales invoices
- [ ] supplier invoices
- [ ] receipts
- [ ] Corporation Tax records
- [ ] VAT records where relevant
- [ ] PAYE records where relevant
- [ ] accountant engagement

## Trading evidence

- [ ] website
- [ ] domain ownership
- [ ] business email
- [ ] client agreements
- [ ] client invoices
- [ ] proof of client payments
- [ ] delivery records
- [ ] reports
- [ ] marketing campaigns
- [ ] CRM activity
- [ ] proposals/quotations

## Staff and contractors

- [ ] role descriptions
- [ ] signed agreements
- [ ] payment records
- [ ] onboarding records
- [ ] performance records
- [ ] right-to-work records where legally required
- [ ] payroll records where relevant

## Operations

- [ ] organisation chart
- [ ] SOP library
- [ ] staff list
- [ ] client list
- [ ] task records
- [ ] quality-control process
- [ ] complaints/escalations process

## Compliance

- [ ] ICO/data protection
- [ ] privacy policy
- [ ] cookie policy
- [ ] insurance
- [ ] terms and conditions
- [ ] health and safety where relevant
- [ ] employment-law compliance where relevant

## Rule

Keep genuine records as part of ordinary business operation.

Never create retrospective fake evidence for a bank, regulator, accountant or immigration application.
