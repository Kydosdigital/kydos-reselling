# Social Media Client Onboarding Form

Use this structure to build the Google Form.

## Section 1, Business details

1. Business name
2. Legal company name, if different
3. Website
4. Industry
5. Business location
6. Areas served
7. Main contact name
8. Main contact email
9. Main contact phone
10. Preferred contact method

## Section 2, Business goals

11. What do you want social media to help you achieve?

Options can include:
- brand awareness
- enquiries/leads
- website traffic
- followers
- community
- sales
- recruitment
- event promotion
- authority/trust
- other

12. What is the single most important goal?

13. Are there any current campaigns, offers or launches?

## Section 3, Target audience

14. Who is your ideal customer?
15. Typical age range
16. Gender, if relevant
17. Locations
18. Customer type, B2B/B2C/both
19. What problems do they have?
20. What do they care about?
21. What usually stops them buying?

## Section 4, Services and products

22. List your main services/products.
23. Which service/product should we prioritise?
24. Are prices public?
25. Are there offers or promotions?
26. Are there services we should not promote?

## Section 5, Brand

27. Describe your tone of voice.
28. List words/phrases you like.
29. List words/phrases you do not want used.
30. Brand colours
31. Fonts
32. Upload logo
33. Upload brand guidelines
34. Upload previous designs

## Section 6, Content

35. What topics should we cover?
36. What topics should we avoid?
37. What type of content do you like?
38. What type of content do you dislike?
39. Share 3 accounts/brands whose content you like.
40. Why do you like them?
41. What previous content has performed well?
42. What previous content performed badly?

## Section 7, Competitors

43. Competitor 1
44. Competitor 2
45. Competitor 3
46. What do you think they do well?
47. What should make your brand different?

## Section 8, Social accounts

48. Instagram
49. Facebook
50. TikTok
51. LinkedIn
52. X
53. Google Business Profile
54. Other

## Section 9, Access

55. Which accounts still need access to be granted?
56. Who currently owns/administers the accounts?
57. Upload or provide access through the approved secure process.

Do not encourage plain-text password sharing where delegated access is available.

## Section 10, Assets

58. Do you have:
- photography
- video
- testimonials
- team images
- product images
- location images
- event footage
- customer-generated content

59. Upload or place all assets in the client Drive.

## Section 11, Approval

60. Who approves content?
61. Approval email
62. Approval phone/WhatsApp
63. Is anyone else required to approve?

## Section 12, Compliance

64. Is your industry regulated?
65. Are there words/claims we must not make?
66. Are there mandatory disclaimers?
67. Are there legal/compliance approvals required before posting?

## Section 13, Important dates

68. Upcoming events
69. Launches
70. Promotions
71. Anniversaries
72. Seasonal dates
73. Any dates the business does not want to post

## Section 14, Final notes

74. Is there anything else your Account Manager should know?
