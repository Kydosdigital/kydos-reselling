# Client Google Drive Folder Structure

Create one top-level folder per client:

[Client Name]

Inside create:

## 01 Onboarding

Store:
- signed agreement
- onboarding form export
- client contacts
- package/scope
- onboarding checklist
- Content Rules Sheet

## 02 Brand Assets

Store:
- logos
- fonts
- colour references
- brand guidelines
- approved photos
- approved video
- templates

## 03 Content Calendars

Suggested structure:

2026/
- January
- February
- March
- etc.

Store:
- draft calendar
- reviewed calendar
- client-approved calendar

## 04 Designs

Suggested:

2026/
Month/
Week 1
Week 2
Week 3
Week 4

Use clear final/draft status naming.

## 05 Videos

Same monthly/weekly structure.

## 06 Approvals

Store:
- approval screenshots
- approval emails
- revision log
- final sign-off

## 07 Reports

Monthly reports.

## 08 Ads

Where applicable:
- briefs
- creative
- copy
- reports
- landing-page references

## 09 Website and SEO

Where applicable:
- website briefs
- SEO research
- copy
- audits
- reports

## 10 Account Access

This folder should contain:
- access instructions
- platform ownership notes
- delegated-access records

Do not use it as an unprotected plain-text password vault.

Use delegated access and an approved password manager where credentials must be stored.

## Naming rule

Use:

YYYY-MM-DD - Description - Status

Example:

2026-09-03 - September Calendar - APPROVED

Avoid:
final-final-v2-new-final.pdf
