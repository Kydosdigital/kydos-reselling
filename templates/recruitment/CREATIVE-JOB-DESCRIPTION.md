# Creative Job Description

Role:
Creative

Reports to:
Operations Manager, with day-to-day briefs from Account Managers

## Role purpose

Produce professional visual content from approved Account Manager briefs.

## Responsibilities

- static social media design
- carousel design
- Story design
- reel/video editing
- subtitles
- visual adaptation across platforms
- correct export sizes
- source-file organisation
- brand consistency
- image/video asset selection
- correction rounds

## Current Kydos-style tools may include

- CapCut
- Adobe Photoshop
- Adobe Creative Cloud
- Canva
- Freepik
- Envato

The tool matters less than the professional quality of the output.

## Capacity

Normal:
4 client accounts

Upper working capacity:
5 client accounts

## Required skills

- strong visual hierarchy
- professional social design
- video editing
- typography
- spacing
- colour use
- mobile-first readability
- ability to follow brand guidelines
- ability to interpret a written Creative brief
- attention to spelling and detail

## Working deadline

Weekly client batches:
Monday, 1:00 PM UK time.

If the brief is late or required assets are missing, raise it early.

## Hiring process

- portfolio review
- screening interview
- unpaid test task
- final review
- 3-month probation

Compensation:
Provided during recruitment. Contact Kydos for current recruitment support and budget guidance.
