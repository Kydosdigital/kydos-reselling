# Operations Manager Job Description

Role:
Operations Manager

Reports to:
Agency Owner

## Role purpose

Run the day-to-day delivery operation so the owner does not need to personally manage every client task.

## Responsibilities

- manage Account Managers and Creatives
- monitor deadlines
- review monthly content calendars
- oversee client delivery quality
- check workload and capacity
- resolve team blockers
- escalate serious client issues
- monitor overdue approvals
- monitor overdue payments with the relevant owner/finance process
- check monthly reports
- maintain SOP compliance
- oversee staff onboarding
- conduct probation/performance reviews
- maintain delivery trackers
- report operational performance to the owner

## Quality control

The Operations Manager checks:

- calendar quality
- client-rule compliance
- repeated errors
- missed deadlines
- weak copy
- poor Creative quality
- outstanding client issues
- client churn risk

The Operations Manager is not expected to rewrite every caption or redesign every post.

The job is to make sure the right people are doing their jobs correctly.

## Daily priorities

1. urgent client issues
2. missed or at-risk deadlines
3. approvals
4. Creative bottlenecks
5. staff blockers
6. escalations
7. owner report

## Weekly priorities

- workload review
- client delivery review
- quality review
- staffing review
- upcoming deadline review
- problem-account review

## Monthly priorities

- review client reports
- review team performance
- review client capacity
- review churn risk
- review recurring errors
- recommend hiring or process changes

## Required skills

- strong agency operations judgment
- people management
- quality control
- organisation
- communication
- understanding of content and digital marketing workflows
- ability to challenge weak work
- ability to report clearly to the owner

## Hiring process

- screening
- operational scenario interview
- final interview
- 3-month probation
