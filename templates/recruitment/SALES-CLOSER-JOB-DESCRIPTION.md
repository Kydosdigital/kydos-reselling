# Sales Closer Job Description

Role:
Sales Closer

Reports to:
Agency Owner or Operations/Sales Lead

## Role purpose

Respond quickly to qualified agency enquiries, understand the prospect's needs and close the appropriate service package.

## Responsibilities

- call fresh leads quickly
- use the CRM
- qualify prospects
- book discovery calls
- recommend suitable packages
- handle objections professionally
- follow up open opportunities
- maintain accurate pipeline stages
- record notes and next actions
- send or trigger payment links
- send or trigger agreements
- hand paid clients into onboarding

## Core KPI

Speed to lead:
Target fresh lead call within 60 seconds where operationally possible.

Other useful KPIs:
- contact rate
- discovery calls booked
- show rate
- close rate
- revenue closed
- follow-up completion
- pipeline hygiene

## Commission model

Recurring services:
50% of the first service fee closed, excluding VAT.

One-off projects:
Use the approved one-off commission rate in the signed Sales Closer Agreement. Kydos's current working model is approximately 15% for one-off work such as websites.

Commission should be based on money received, not verbal promises.

## Required skills

- confident phone communication
- listening
- qualification
- objection handling
- accurate CRM use
- clear follow-up
- commercial judgment
- honesty

## Not allowed

- guaranteeing results
- inventing discounts
- misrepresenting package inclusions
- promising delivery dates without authority
- leaving CRM notes incomplete
