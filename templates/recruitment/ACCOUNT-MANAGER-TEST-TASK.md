# Account Manager Test Task

This is an unpaid recruitment assessment.

It must not be used as live client work.

## Scenario

You have been assigned a fictional UK business:

Name:
Northline Physio

Location:
Manchester

Services:
Sports injury rehabilitation, back pain treatment, post-operative rehabilitation

Audience:
Adults aged 25 to 55 in Greater Manchester

Goal:
Increase enquiries and establish trust

Platforms:
Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn

## Task

Create one week of content consisting of 3 posts.

For each post provide:

- platform
- format
- objective
- content pillar
- hook
- full caption
- CTA
- visual direction

Then provide:

1. three competitors or reference accounts you would research
2. five questions you would ask the client before building the full month
3. one example of a claim you would verify before publishing

## What we assess

- research thinking
- relevance
- copy quality
- hooks
- CTA quality
- strategic variety
- client awareness
- factual caution
- grammar
- professionalism

## Time expectation

The task should be proportionate.

Do not ask the candidate to produce a full month of usable client work.
