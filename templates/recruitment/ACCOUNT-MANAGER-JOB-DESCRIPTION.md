# Account Manager Job Description

Role:
Account Manager

Reports to:
Operations Manager or Agency Owner

## Role purpose

Own assigned client accounts from strategy through delivery coordination, client communication and monthly reporting.

## Responsibilities

- understand each client's business, audience and goals
- conduct competitor and content research
- create monthly content calendars
- write captions, hooks, CTAs and social copy
- manage client communication
- collect approvals
- record client corrections
- create weekly Creative briefs
- review Creative output before client submission
- schedule/publish approved content
- manage Stories where included
- manage community engagement where included
- prepare monthly reports
- identify risks, delays and upsell opportunities
- maintain client files and account notes

## Capacity

Normal:
4 client accounts

Upper working capacity:
5 client accounts

## Required skills

- excellent written English
- strong copywriting
- strong research
- ability to build a strategic social media calendar
- understanding of hooks and CTAs
- attention to detail
- professional client communication
- ability to manage deadlines
- understanding of major social platforms
- ability to give clear Creative briefs

## Strong candidates can

- explain why a post exists
- adapt tone between industries
- identify weak generic content
- distinguish strategy from activity
- handle client corrections without defensiveness
- turn client feedback into permanent account rules

## Useful experience

- agency environment
- social media management
- client services
- copywriting
- content strategy
- reporting

## Working standard

- full-time working relationship unless stated otherwise
- UK-aligned delivery deadlines
- weekly Creative handover deadline process
- monthly calendar deadline by the 25th
- confidentiality and client-data obligations

## Hiring process

- application review
- screening interview
- unpaid test task
- final interview
- 3-month probation
