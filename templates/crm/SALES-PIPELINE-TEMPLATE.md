# Sales Pipeline Template

## Stage 1, New Lead

Definition:
New enquiry captured and not yet spoken to.

Required action:
Call within 60 seconds where possible.

Exit condition:
Contact attempted.

## Stage 2, Contacted

Definition:
Salesperson has attempted or made contact.

Required:
Record call outcome and next action.

## Stage 3, Qualified

Definition:
Prospect has a genuine business need, can use the service and is worth progressing.

Record:
- service interest
- budget
- decision-maker
- urgency
- main problem
- next step

## Stage 4, Discovery Call Booked

Definition:
Call is booked.

Automations:
- confirmation
- reminder
- internal notification

## Stage 5, Proposal / Offer Sent

Definition:
Package or quotation has been presented.

Record:
- package
- price
- VAT
- one-off/recurring
- expected decision date

## Stage 6, Follow-Up

Definition:
Prospect has not yet said yes or no.

Set:
- next follow-up date
- reason for delay
- objection

## Stage 7, Verbal Yes

Definition:
Prospect has agreed in principle.

Immediate actions:
- invoice/payment link
- agreement

## Stage 8, Paid / Won

Definition:
Payment received.

Actions:
- client tag
- onboarding email
- client folder
- Account Manager assignment
- onboarding task

## Stage 9, Lost

Record reason:

- price
- timing
- no response
- competitor
- not suitable
- no budget
- internal decision
- other

## Stage 10, Nurture

Use for prospects who are not ready now but may become viable later.

Every opportunity must have:
- owner
- stage
- next action
- last contact date
