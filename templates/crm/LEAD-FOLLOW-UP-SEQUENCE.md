# Lead Follow-Up Sequence

This sequence is a default framework. Adjust frequency based on replies, consent, source and client context.

## Immediately

- email confirmation
- SMS confirmation
- WhatsApp confirmation where configured
- sales call

## Same day

If no answer:
- second call later at a sensible time
- short message

## Day 1

- call attempt
- short follow-up

## Day 3

- follow-up email or message
- restate the problem/offer
- simple booking link

## Day 5

- final active follow-up in the short sequence

Example:

"Hi [Name], just checking in on your enquiry about [service]. If this is still something you want to look at, I'm happy to talk it through. If the timing isn't right, just let me know and I won't keep chasing you."

## After short sequence

Move to:
- nurture
- lost
- future follow-up

Do not leave a lead permanently in "New Lead".

## Stop rules

Stop active sales follow-up when:
- prospect asks to stop
- number/email is invalid
- business is unsuitable
- prospect clearly declines
- legal/consent requirements require stopping

## CRM requirement

Every open lead must have:
- pipeline stage
- assigned salesperson
- next action
- next action date
