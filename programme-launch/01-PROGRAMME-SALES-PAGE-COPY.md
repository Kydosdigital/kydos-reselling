## Supporting line

This is not a generic "start an agency" course.

You receive the actual operating structure needed to launch and run a real digital marketing agency, including company setup guidance, pricing, sales systems, client onboarding, recruitment, fulfilment SOPs, finance tools, CRM processes and ready-to-use templates.

## Who this is for

This programme is designed for people who want to:

- start a UK digital marketing agency from zero
- add a structured digital agency business to their existing experience
- build a company that can eventually operate with a team instead of depending entirely on the owner
- learn how to sell and manage digital services without personally becoming the specialist for every service
- build proper sales, fulfilment, reporting and management systems from the beginning

## This may not be for you if

- you are looking for guaranteed income
- you want a "get rich quick" programme
- you do not want to manage or build a genuine business
- you expect Kydos to guarantee clients
- you want immigration advice rather than a business programme
- you are unwilling to invest in normal business costs such as software, staff or advertising
