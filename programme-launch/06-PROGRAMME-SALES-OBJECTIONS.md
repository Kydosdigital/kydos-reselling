# Programme Sales Objection Handling

## "£2,500 is expensive for a course"

Response:

"That makes sense if you compare it with a video course. This is structured as an agency operating system. It includes the company and finance setup process, CRM, sales, client onboarding, delivery SOPs, recruitment, contracts, templates, calculators and active support. The higher tiers also include implementation."

## "Can I pay monthly?"

Current Kydos policy:

No standard instalment plan.

"The programme is paid in full before access or implementation starts."

## "Can you guarantee I will get clients?"

"No. We can give you the client-acquisition system, sales process and launch structure, but we do not guarantee a number of clients or revenue."

## "Can I make £10,000 a month?"

Do not validate income promises.

"You can build toward whatever commercial target makes sense for your business, but we do not promise a revenue figure. The finance tools help you model clients, margins and hiring based on real numbers."

## "Why can't I just watch YouTube?"

"You absolutely can learn individual skills for free. What this programme is designed to solve is the fragmentation. Instead of working out company setup, CRM, packages, contracts, recruitment, client onboarding, content delivery, reporting, finances and operations separately, the full system is structured for you."

## "I don't know digital marketing"

"The owner does not have to become the specialist for every service. You do need to understand what the agency sells and how to manage quality. Specialist fulfilment can be handled by the right staff or contractors."

## "I don't want to manage staff"

"That is exactly why the programme teaches an Operations Manager structure. The £10,000 option is the strongest fit if you want Kydos to recruit and train that initial management layer."

## "Is the website included?"

Blueprint:
No.

Build With Us:
Yes, one standard 5-page website.

Done For You:
Yes, one standard 5-page website.

## "Is advertising included?"

Campaign setup/support depends on tier.

Ad spend is separate.

## "Do you charge me for the CRM?"

Kydos does not add an extra programme licence fee for the participant's own agency CRM setup.

Third-party provider and usage costs may apply.

Use the approved referral route where required.

## "Can this help with a sponsor licence?"

"The programme can help you build and properly document a genuine business. We have an optional business-readiness module, but sponsor-licence and personal visa eligibility are regulated immigration matters. We refer those questions to an immigration solicitor/adviser and never guarantee an outcome."

## "Can I get a refund if I change my mind?"

Use only the solicitor-approved Refund and Cancellation Policy.

Do not improvise or say "no refunds under any circumstances."
