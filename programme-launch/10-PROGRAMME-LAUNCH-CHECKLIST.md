# Programme Commercial Launch Checklist

This is the checklist for Kydos launching and selling the programme itself.

- [ ] final programme name

- [ ] full-payment policy
- [ ] support periods

## Launch gate

Do not launch paid acquisition until:

- checkout works
- agreement/consent works
- CRM works
- salesperson is ready

- portal access works
- active support owner exists
