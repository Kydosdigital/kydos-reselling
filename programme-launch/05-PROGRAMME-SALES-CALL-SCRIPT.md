# Programme Sales Call Script

## Opening

"Hi [Name], it's [Name] from Kydos Digital. You recently asked about our programme for building your own digital marketing agency. Have you got a few minutes?"

## Understand the goal

"What made you interested in starting an agency?"

Then ask naturally:

- Are you starting from zero or already offering services?
- Do you already have a UK company?
- Do you want to run the agency day to day, or eventually have a manager run operations?
- Do you want to personally deliver the services, or mainly own/manage the business?
- When do you want to launch?
- What have you already set up?
- What is your biggest concern about starting?

## Explain the model

"The programme is built around a real agency operating structure, not just marketing theory. We cover the company, branding, website, CRM, client acquisition, sales, onboarding, delivery team, reporting and operations."

## Tier recommendation

### Blueprint

Recommend when:

- they are hands-on
- comfortable implementing systems
- want the templates and roadmap
- budget is £2,500

### Build With Us

Recommend when:

- they want the infrastructure built with help
- need website/brand/CRM implementation
- want recruitment support
- budget is £5,000

### Done For You

Recommend when:

- they want a much more complete setup
- want an Operations Manager model
- want initial team recruitment/training
- want handover
- budget is £10,000

## Price explanation

"The programme is paid in full upfront. The fee is for the programme and the implementation included in your tier. Normal third-party business costs such as advertising, software, staff, accountant and provider fees are separate where applicable."

## Four-week wording

"Our default launch roadmap is built around four weeks, but that is a target. Things such as bank checks, company-provider processing, recruitment and how quickly you respond can change the actual timeline."

## Results wording

"We don't guarantee that you will immediately get clients or a particular income. What we build is the operating system and, depending on tier, the actual infrastructure and launch team needed to run the agency."

## Immigration question

If prospect asks:

"Can I use this to sponsor myself?"

Do not answer eligibility.

Say:

"The programme builds the business itself. We also have an optional business-readiness section, but personal immigration and sponsor-licence eligibility has to be handled by our regulated immigration adviser or your own adviser. We do not sell a guaranteed visa outcome."

## Close

"If you're happy with [Tier], the next step is payment and the Participant Agreement. Once that is completed, we send your intake and set your programme start and support dates."

## After yes

- update CRM
- confirm tier
- send payment
- send agreement/checkout consent
- confirm successful payment
- trigger intake
- assign programme owner
