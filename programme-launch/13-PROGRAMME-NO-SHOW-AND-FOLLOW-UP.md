# Programme No-Show and Follow-Up Process

## No-show

If prospect misses the consultation:

1. mark No Show
2. send short email/SMS
3. provide reschedule link
4. make one sensible call attempt
5. set follow-up task

## No-show message

Hi {{first_name}},

We missed you on your Kydos Digital agency programme consultation today.

If you still want to look at building your agency, you can choose another time here:

[Reschedule Link]

If the timing is no longer right, just let us know and we will update our notes.

Kydos Digital

## After consultation, no decision

### Same day

Send:

- recommended tier
- price
- key inclusions
- payment/next-step link
- any requested information

### Day 2

Short follow-up:

"Hi [Name], just checking you received the information for the [Tier] option we discussed. Is there anything you want me to clarify before you decide?"

### Day 5

Address the real objection.

### Day 10

Final active follow-up:

"Hi [Name], I wanted to close the loop on the agency programme. If you still want to move forward, I am happy to help. If the timing is not right, let me know and we can stop chasing and reconnect later."

## Nurture

Move prospects who are genuinely interested but not ready into Nurture.

Suggested future touchpoints:

- programme update
- useful agency launch guide
- invitation to consultation
- new cohort/availability where genuine
- relevant Kydos case-study/behind-the-scenes content

Do not manufacture false deadlines.
