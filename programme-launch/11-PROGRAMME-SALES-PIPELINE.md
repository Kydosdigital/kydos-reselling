# Programme Sales Pipeline

## Stage 1, New Programme Lead

Definition:
New enquiry captured.

Immediate actions:

- email
- SMS
- WhatsApp where configured
- salesperson notification
- rapid call

Required fields:

- lead source
- country
- tier interest
- start timeframe
- minimum investment readiness

## Stage 2, Contacted

Salesperson has attempted contact.

Record:

- answered?
- basic fit
- next action

## Stage 3, Qualified

Prospect is a credible programme buyer.

Qualify around:

- genuine business goal
- programme fit
- implementation expectation
- ability to invest
- launch timing
- owner-operated vs hands-off preference

Do not qualify on immigration status.

## Stage 4, Consultation Booked

Appointment confirmed.

Automations:

- email confirmation
- SMS reminder
- salesperson alert
- appointment task

## Stage 5, Consultation Completed

Record:

- recommended tier
- concerns
- decision timeline
- professional referral need
- next action

## Stage 6, Payment / Agreement Sent

Record:

- tier
- programme fee
- VAT
- agreement version
- checkout link
- sent date

## Stage 7, Follow-Up

Prospect has not yet paid.

Every opportunity must have:

- reason
- next contact date
- salesperson
- tier

## Stage 8, Paid / Participant

Payment and required agreement/consent process completed.

Actions:

- participant tag
- onboarding automation
- intake
- portal
- programme owner assignment

## Stage 9, Not Ready / Nurture

Use when:

- interested but timing later
- funding not ready
- business decision postponed

Set future follow-up date.

## Stage 10, Lost

Record reason:

- budget
- timing
- decided not to start
- chose competitor/alternative
- no response
- not suitable
- wanted guaranteed income
- immigration-only interest
- other

Do not leave opportunities indefinitely in New Lead or Follow-Up.
