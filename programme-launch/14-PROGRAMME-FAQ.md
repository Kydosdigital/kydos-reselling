# Programme FAQ

## What is the Kydos Digital agency programme?

It is a structured system for building and running your own digital marketing agency using the processes behind Kydos Digital.

It covers company setup, branding, website, CRM, sales, recruitment, client onboarding, social media delivery, specialist services, operations, reporting, finance and retention.

## Is this a franchise?

No.

Your agency is your own independent business.

## Is this white labelling?

The programme itself is not a white-label agency-reselling model.

You own and operate your own agency.

The programme may help you configure your own branded CRM through an approved provider/referral route.

## Can I pay in instalments?

The standard programme payment model is full payment upfront.

## Is there a free trial?

No.

## How quickly can I launch?

The default roadmap targets approximately 4 weeks.

An 8-week or 12-week pace is also possible.

Third-party processing, recruitment and participant delays can affect the actual launch date.

## Do I need marketing experience?

No specific background is required, but you must be willing to understand the services your agency sells and the standards required.

You do not need to personally fulfil every specialist service.

## Do I need to be a designer?

No.

The model separates Account Management and Creative production.

## Do I have to run Meta Ads myself?

No.

The programme teaches you how to scope, sell, manage and quality-control specialist services. A competent specialist contractor can fulfil the technical work.

## Is a website included?

Blueprint:
No.

Build With Us:
Yes, one standard 5-page website.

Done For You:
Yes, one standard 5-page website.

## Do I get a CRM?

The programme helps you set up your own branded agency CRM.

Kydos does not add a separate programme CRM licence fee, but third-party/provider and usage fees can apply.

## Will Kydos find staff for me?

Blueprint:
You receive the recruitment system.

Build With Us:
Recruitment support is included.

Done For You:
Kydos recruits/sources and trains the agreed initial launch team.

## What staff do I need before I advertise?

Recommended minimum:

- Account Manager
- Creative
- Sales Closer

For a more hands-off structure:

- Operations Manager

## How much advertising budget do I need?

For the participant's own agency launch, Kydos recommends starting with at least £300 for the initial Meta Ads test.

This is separate from the programme fee.

## Do you guarantee clients?

No.

## Do you guarantee revenue?

No.

## Do you guarantee a sponsor licence or visa?

No.

There is no formal immigration route called "self-sponsorship".

The programme can help you build a genuine, properly organised business and includes optional business-readiness information.

Personal sponsor-licence and immigration eligibility must be assessed by a regulated immigration adviser/solicitor.

## Can I use the same accountant as Kydos?

Kydos can make an introduction where appropriate.

The accountant is an independent professional and agrees fees/scope directly with the participant unless otherwise stated.

## Do I keep the templates?

The commercial intention is broad use rights and planned lifetime material access, subject to the final Participant Agreement and Template Use Licence.

## Can I resell the templates?

The draft Template Use Licence is designed to allow broad commercial adaptation and resale of adapted templates, subject to protecting Kydos branding, confidential information and third-party rights.

Final legal wording requires solicitor approval.

## What happens after I pay?

You complete the programme intake, receive portal access, get your launch roadmap and support dates, and your build begins according to your tier.
