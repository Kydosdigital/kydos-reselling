# Kydos Programme Meta Ads Launch Plan

Purpose:

Generate qualified consultation enquiries from people interested in building a UK digital marketing agency.

## Campaign objective

Primary recommendation:

Lead generation.

Test:

- Meta Instant Form
- landing-page conversion

Start simple and optimise from actual lead quality.

## Initial Kydos test budget

Recommended programme launch test:

£20 to £30 per day for an initial controlled test.

This is a Kydos marketing budget recommendation for selling the programme, not the £300 startup advertising budget recommended to programme participants for their own agency launch.

## Audience approach

Start broad enough to let Meta learn.

Potential signals to test:

- entrepreneurship
- business ownership
- digital marketing
- social media marketing
- freelancers
- marketing professionals
- people interested in starting businesses
- business services

Avoid over-narrowing before lead data exists.

## Geography

Initial:

United Kingdom.

Secondary tests later:

- UK-based international professionals
- selected overseas markets where the programme offer, company-formation logistics and legal wording are suitable

Do not target immigration vulnerability with guaranteed visa claims.

## Form questions

Keep qualification useful but not excessive.

1. Full name
2. Email
3. Phone
4. Where are you currently based?
5. Do you already have a UK company?
6. Which option interests you?
   - £2,500 Blueprint
   - £5,000 Build With Us
   - £10,000 Done For You
   - Not sure
7. When would you like to start?
   - Immediately
   - Within 30 days
   - 1 to 3 months
   - Researching
8. Are you in a position to invest at least £2,500 into building the agency?
   - Yes
   - Not yet

Do not ask immigration-status questions in the ad form unless there is a clear lawful and necessary reason.

## CRM flow

Meta lead

-> CRM contact

-> Programme Lead tag

-> programme-sales pipeline

-> email

-> SMS

-> WhatsApp where configured

-> sales notification

-> rapid call

-> consultation

-> payment

-> Participant Agreement/consents

-> programme intake

## Lead quality feedback

Track:

- answer rate
- qualified rate
- tier interest
- payment readiness
- consultation booked
- attended
- closed
- revenue
- reason lost

Optimise for qualified commercial intent, not cheapest form completion.
