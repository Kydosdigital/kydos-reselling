# Programme Lost Lead Reasons

Use consistent reasons in CRM.

## Budget

Prospect cannot currently invest at least the entry price.

## Timing

Interested but not ready to start.

Move to nurture where appropriate.

## No response

Reasonable follow-up sequence completed with no response.

## Decided not to start a business

No longer pursuing an agency.

## Chose another course/provider

Record competitor only if known.

## Wanted guaranteed clients/income

Programme expectation mismatch.

## Immigration-only interest

Prospect mainly wanted a guaranteed sponsor/visa route rather than the business programme.

Do not attempt to close using immigration promises.

## Existing agency does not need programme

May be better fit for consulting/another Kydos service.

## Not suitable / conduct

Examples:

- fraudulent intent
- wants fake records
- abusive behaviour
- prohibited/unlawful business use

## Other

Use notes.

## Reporting

Monthly, review:

- lost by reason
- tier most requested
- budget objections
- timing objections
- consultation-to-close rate
- lead source quality

Use this to improve positioning, not to pressure unsuitable prospects.
