## Executive summary

This month focused on building trust around injury rehabilitation and explaining the clinic's assessment process.

The strongest content was practical education delivered through short video and carousel formats.

Reach improved, but website clicks did not increase at the same rate. Next month we should connect more educational posts directly to service pages and booking.

## What we learnt

1. Real clinician video performs better than stock footage.
2. Practical carousels generate the most saves.
3. Educational reach is growing faster than booking clicks.

## Recommendations

1. Add service-linked CTA to one educational post each week.
2. Record two new clinician videos.
3. Test a patient FAQ series.
