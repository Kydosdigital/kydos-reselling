Last updated:

## Positioning

What the business wants to be known for:

Evidence-aware physiotherapy that helps people return confidently to normal activity.

Primary audience:

Adults aged 25 to 55 in Greater Manchester.

Priority services:

- sports injury rehabilitation
- back pain treatment
- post-operative rehabilitation

Priority geography:

Manchester and Greater Manchester.

## Tone

Use:

- calm
- practical
- reassuring
- professional
- easy-to-understand explanations

Avoid:

- dramatic health language
- shaming people for inactivity
- overpromising
- generic motivational quotes

## Approved wording

- assessment
- rehabilitation plan
- physiotherapy
- personalised treatment
- return to activity

## Forbidden wording

Do not say:

- "We cure back pain"
- "Guaranteed recovery"
- "Fix your injury in one session"
- "You definitely have..."

## Content rules

Prioritise:

- common misconceptions
- what assessment involves
- rehabilitation education
- clinician expertise
- realistic progress
- client experience without exaggerated results

Claims requiring checking:

- prevalence statistics
- clinical outcome percentages
- claims about treatment superiority

## Visual rules

Preferred:

- real clinic photography
- staff imagery
- clean diagrams
- minimal text
- navy and neutral palette

Avoid:

- dramatic stock images of people clutching their back
- red pain graphics
- AI-generated fake patients
- excessive medical imagery

## CTA rules

Preferred:

- Book an assessment
- Speak to the clinic
- Learn more about our physiotherapy services

Avoid:

- "Get cured today"
- "Last chance" unless a genuine deadline exists

## Client correction history

Date:
14 September 2026

Correction:
Client asked us not to use the phrase "pain-free in weeks".

Permanent rule:
Never promise a timeframe for recovery unless it is a direct, properly contextualised Client-approved factual statement.
