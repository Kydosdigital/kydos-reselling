# SEO Audit Worked Example

Client:
Northline Physio Ltd

Website:
https://example.co.uk

Date:
31 August 2026

## Executive summary

Overall condition:
The site is technically accessible but has weak service-page targeting and limited internal linking.

Biggest opportunity:
Build stronger Manchester-focused service pages around high-intent physiotherapy searches.

Biggest risk:
Several services currently compete on one generic page, making search intent unclear.

Priority action:
Separate and strengthen the Back Pain, Sports Injury and Post-Operative Rehabilitation pages.

## Indexation

Good:
Core pages indexed.

Issue:
Old test page remains indexed.

Action:
Remove or redirect test page.

## Metadata

Issue:
Homepage title is only "Northline Physio".

Recommendation:
Use a clearer title connecting brand, primary service and location.

## Headings

Issue:
Two service pages have no clear H1.

Action:
Create one descriptive H1 per page.

## Content

Problem:
Back Pain page has fewer than 200 words and no explanation of assessment or treatment process.

Recommendation:
Expand useful service information rather than padding with generic text.

## Keyword targeting

Primary themes:

- physiotherapist Manchester
- back pain physio Manchester
- sports injury physio Manchester
- post operative physio Manchester

Cannibalisation:
Homepage and Services page both target generic Manchester physiotherapy.

## Internal links

Add links from educational articles to:

- Back Pain
- Sports Injury
- Book Assessment

## Technical

Mobile:
Good.

Speed:
Image-heavy homepage hero needs compression.

Structured data:
LocalBusiness/MedicalBusiness review recommended.

## Local SEO

Google Business Profile:
Active.

Opportunity:
Improve service descriptions and systematically request genuine reviews.

## Tracking

GA4:
Installed.

Search Console:
Installed.

Conversion:
Booking form event not configured.

## Priority action plan

### Critical

1. Configure booking conversion.
2. Remove indexed test page.

### High

1. Rebuild three priority service pages.
2. Improve titles/H1 structure.
3. Compress homepage images.

### Medium

1. Add internal linking.
2. Improve Google Business Profile services.

### Later

1. Build FAQ content from real patient questions.
2. Develop local content around rehabilitation and return-to-sport themes.
