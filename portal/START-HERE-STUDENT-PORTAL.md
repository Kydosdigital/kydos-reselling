# Start Here

Welcome to the Kydos Digital Agency Business-in-a-Box.

This portal is your operating manual for building and running your own digital marketing agency.

## Step 1, Know your tier

### Blueprint

You are implementing the system.

Use:

- guides
- templates
- calculators
- videos
- WhatsApp support

### Build With Us

Kydos is helping implement the core infrastructure.

You will have participant actions and Kydos actions running at the same time.

### Done For You Agency

Kydos is coordinating the core build, initial team recruitment/training and handover.

You still need to make owner decisions and complete third-party verification when required.

## Step 2, Choose your pace

Default:

4 weeks.

Alternative:

8 weeks.

12 weeks.

Your pace should match:

- availability
- third-party processing
- recruitment
- budget
- existing setup

## Step 3, Complete your intake

Do not skip this.

Your intake tells us:

- what exists already
- what needs to be built
- what services you want
- how hands-on you want to be
- what your launch dependencies are

- professional email
- CRM

- client agreement
- onboarding
- Account Manager
- Creative
- Sales Closer
- delivery workflow
