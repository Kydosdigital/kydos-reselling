# Support End Automations

## Data fields

- tier
- start date
- support end date
- handover date
- support status

## 14 days before

Internal notification:

"[Participant] has 14 days of active support remaining."

Review:

- open blockers
- unfinished implementation
- professional referrals
- outstanding participant actions

## 7 days before

Participant message:

"Your active Kydos programme support period ends on [date]. Your programme materials remain available according to your access terms. If there is anything in your current implementation you want us to clarify before active support ends, please send it through this week."

## 2 days before

Internal:

Check unresolved issues.

Do not create artificial urgency or withhold contracted work until the end.

## End date

Update:

Support Status = Alumni / Materials Access

Send:

"Your active support period has now ended. Your programme materials remain available according to your programme terms. If you need additional hands-on implementation, recruitment or consulting later, you can contact Kydos for a separate quotation."

## Done For You

Do not calculate 90-day support from payment date.

Calculate from formal handover date.

## Extended support

If Kydos later creates a paid extended-support plan:

- separate product
- separate scope
- separate support end
- no automatic billing without agreement
