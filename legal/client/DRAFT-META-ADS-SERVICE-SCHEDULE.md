# DRAFT Meta Ads Service Schedule

Attach to the Master Client Service Agreement.

## 1. Management fee

Kydos reference pricing:

- £150/month where monthly Meta ad spend is up to £500
- 15% of monthly ad spend where spend exceeds £500

The final Order Form must state the actual fee calculation.

Ad spend is separate and funded by the Client.

## 2. Scope

May include:

- campaign planning
- campaign setup
- audience configuration
- copy
- Creative coordination
- lead form/landing destination
- tracking setup/check
- CRM handoff check
- ongoing optimisation
- monthly reporting

## 3. Client access

Client must provide suitable access to:

- Meta Business portfolio
- Page
- Instagram
- ad account
- Pixel/dataset where relevant
- domain/website where relevant

## 4. Budget

Client controls/funds the approved media budget.

The Agency will not intentionally exceed the agreed budget settings, but platform billing/delivery can vary and should be monitored.

## 5. Creative and claims

Client is responsible for accuracy of:

- offer
- pricing
- product/service claims
- legal permissions
- testimonials
- regulated statements

The Agency may reject or pause non-compliant instructions.

## 6. Tracking

The Agency will implement or coordinate agreed tracking, but cannot guarantee perfect attribution because of:

- browser/privacy restrictions
- platform modelling
- cookie consent
- CRM gaps
- offline sales
- user behaviour

## 7. Lead quality

The Agency does not guarantee every lead is:

- contactable
- qualified
- genuine
- ready to buy

Campaign optimisation should use sales feedback where available.

## 8. Results

No guarantee of:

- CPL
- number of leads
- sales
- ROAS
- revenue
- account approval

## 9. Platform risk

Meta can:

- reject ads
- restrict accounts
- change policies
- change delivery

The Agency will use reasonable efforts to respond but does not control Meta.

## 10. Client sales follow-up

Where the campaign generates leads, the Client must maintain prompt and competent follow-up unless the Agency is contracted to provide that system.

Poor sales follow-up is not a media-buying defect.
