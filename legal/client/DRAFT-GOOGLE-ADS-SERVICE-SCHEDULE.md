# DRAFT Google Ads Service Schedule

Attach to the Master Client Service Agreement.

## 1. Management fee

Kydos reference pricing:

- £300/month where monthly Google Ads spend is up to £1,000
- 15% of monthly ad spend where spend exceeds £1,000

The Order Form must state the actual fee.

Ad spend is separate.

## 2. Scope

May include:

- account/campaign setup
- keyword research
- campaign structure
- ad copy
- negative keywords
- conversion tracking setup/check
- landing-page recommendations
- ongoing search-term review
- bid/budget optimisation
- monthly reporting

## 3. Client access

Client provides:

- Google Ads
- GA4
- Google Tag Manager where used
- website/CMS where required
- call tracking where used

## 4. Search demand

The Agency does not guarantee that sufficient profitable search demand exists for every niche/location/budget.

## 5. Conversion tracking

Tracking can be affected by:

- consent
- browser restrictions
- website changes
- phone tracking
- CRM gaps
- offline conversions

## 6. Landing pages

Where poor landing-page quality materially limits results, the Agency may recommend separately scoped landing-page work.

## 7. Results

No guarantee of:

- click cost
- conversion cost
- leads
- sales
- rankings
- revenue

## 8. Google policies

Google may reject ads, restrict accounts or change platform rules.

The Agency will use reasonable efforts to manage issues but cannot override Google.
