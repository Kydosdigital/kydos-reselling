# DRAFT Website Development Service Schedule

Attach to the Master Client Service Agreement.

## 1. Standard Kydos website

Reference price:
£500.

Scope:
Any 5 standard business pages selected by the Client.

## 2. Payment

Full payment before work starts unless the Order Form states otherwise.

## 3. Included

Unless otherwise stated:

- responsive website
- standard five-page build
- standard navigation/footer
- contact or booking integration
- basic on-page SEO setup
- basic analytics/tracking readiness
- reasonable image optimisation
- standard mobile QA

## 4. Not included by default

- ecommerce
- membership
- custom software
- bespoke portals
- complex databases
- major third-party integrations
- copy beyond agreed scope
- professional photography
- custom animation
- pages beyond five
- ongoing hosting/support
- major post-launch changes

## 5. Client inputs

Client provides on time:

- business information
- page choices
- logo/brand
- images
- legal/compliance wording
- service information
- approvals
- account/domain access

## 6. Review and revisions

The Order Form should state the project revision allowance.

Recommended standard:
2 consolidated revision rounds.

Agency errors are corrected without using the Client's revision allowance unfairly.

## 7. Timeline

Timeline starts when:

- payment received
- brief complete
- required assets/access received

Client delays extend the timeline.

## 8. Domain and hosting

Ownership and recurring fees should be stated clearly.

The Client should own/control its domain account.

## 9. Third-party software

Plugins, themes, APIs, hosting or SaaS may have separate licences/fees.

## 10. Launch

Launch follows final approval and QA.

## 11. Browser/device support

The Agency will use reasonable efforts for current mainstream browsers/devices.

Legacy browser support is not included unless agreed.

## 12. Post-launch

Defects within agreed scope should be reported promptly.

New features and changes are separately quoted.
