# DRAFT Client Approval and Revision Policy

## 1. Purpose

Clear approvals protect both Client and Agency and prevent avoidable rework.

## 2. Authorised approver

The Client must nominate:

- primary approver
- optional secondary approver

The Agency may pause where conflicting instructions are received from multiple people.

## 3. Review target

Standard Kydos target:
3 days.

This is a target for timely delivery, not an automatic approval deadline.

## 4. Silence

Silence is not approval.

If the Client does not respond:

- the Agency follows up
- scheduled dates may move
- the Agency is not responsible for delay caused by missing approval

## 5. Revision rounds

Standard:
2 consolidated revision rounds for ordinary social content.

A revision round means one consolidated set of feedback.

## 6. Agency errors

Clear Agency mistakes such as:

- typo
- wrong logo
- wrong phone number
- failure to follow approved instruction

should be corrected and not unfairly treated as a Client revision.

## 7. Scope change

A new concept after approval, major redesign, extra deliverable or material new direction may be treated as additional scope.

The Agency should quote before proceeding.

## 8. Approval evidence

Approval can be recorded via:

- email
- CRM
- written WhatsApp message
- signed document
- approved project system

Store material approval evidence.

## 9. Regulated content

Client approval does not remove legal/compliance responsibilities.

The Agency may still refuse content reasonably believed unlawful or misleading.
