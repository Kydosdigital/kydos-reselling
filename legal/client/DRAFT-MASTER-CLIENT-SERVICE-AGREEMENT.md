This agreement is intended primarily for business-to-business services. If the client is acting as a consumer, obtain legal review before relying on these terms.

## 1. Agreement structure

The agreement consists of:

1. this Master Client Service Agreement
2. the Commercial Order Form
3. each applicable Service Schedule
4. approved written variations

If there is a conflict:

- the Commercial Order Form controls price, start date and selected service
- the relevant Service Schedule controls service-specific scope
- this Master Agreement controls general terms

## 2. Services

The Agency will provide the services stated in the applicable Service Schedule using reasonable care and skill.

The Agency may use employees, contractors and specialist suppliers to fulfil the services.

The Agency remains responsible for managing its contracted delivery to the Client.

## 3. Commercial Order Form

The Order Form should state:

- selected service/package
- monthly or one-off fee
- VAT
- start date
- initial paid month where applicable
- fixed term if selected
- billing frequency
- revision allowance
- ad spend or third-party budget
- notice provisions
- key exclusions

## 4. Payment

Recurring services are payable in advance.

One-off projects are payable in full before work starts unless the Order Form states otherwise.

Invoices may be issued through the Agency CRM and paid via Stripe or another approved method.

The Client must keep payment details current.

## 5. Late payment

The Client Payment and Late Payment Policy applies.

The Agency may:

- send reminders
- use a grace period
- suspend work after appropriate notice
- reserve statutory late-payment rights where applicable

Kydos's current operating model uses approximately a 14-day grace period before service consequences, subject to the signed terms.

## 6. Term

The Kydos model allows a first paid month before the Client chooses a 3, 6 or 12-month continuation arrangement.

The Order Form must state the actual term.

A one-month notice period does not automatically create a right to terminate a fixed contractual commitment early unless the Order Form expressly says so.

The solicitor should settle the final renewal and termination mechanics.

## 7. Client responsibilities

The Client must:

- provide accurate information
- provide timely access
- provide assets
- obtain permissions for supplied content
- nominate an authorised approver
- review work promptly
- comply with applicable law
- avoid requesting misleading/unlawful claims
- pay agreed third-party budgets
- maintain suitable sales/operational follow-up where relevant

## 8. Approval

The Client Approval and Revision Policy applies.

Standard Kydos social-content process:

- target review window: 3 days
- two revision rounds
- silence is not automatic approval

Where Client delay affects delivery dates, the Agency is not responsible for the resulting schedule movement.

## 9. Changes and out-of-scope work

Material changes to scope should be documented.

The Agency may issue a separate quote for:

- additional pages
- extra content
- extra revision rounds
- new campaigns
- custom development
- complex integrations
- substantial new automation
- work outside the selected package

## 10. Intellectual property

### Client materials

The Client retains rights in materials it supplies and warrants it has permission to provide them for the services.

### Agency background materials

The Agency retains ownership of:

- pre-existing templates
- SOPs
- methods
- know-how
- reusable code/components
- internal systems

### Bespoke deliverables

Ownership/licence of bespoke final deliverables should be specified in the Service Schedule or Order Form.

Third-party licensed assets remain subject to their original licences.

## 11. Portfolio use

The Agency may only use Client work, name, logo, results or testimonial in its portfolio/case studies where the agreed permission clause allows it.

Sensitive/confidential client information must not be disclosed.

## 12. Confidentiality

Each party must keep the other's non-public business information confidential except where disclosure is:

- authorised
- required to perform the contract
- legally required

## 13. Data protection

Each party must comply with applicable data-protection law.

Where the Agency processes personal data on behalf of the Client, appropriate data-processing provisions should be included.

The parties should identify whether each is acting as controller, processor or independent controller for relevant activities.

## 14. Account access

The Client Access and Credentials Policy applies.

Use delegated access where possible.

Do not require the Client to share online-banking passwords, primary email passwords or MFA codes.

## 15. Third-party platforms

The services may depend on:

- Meta
- Google
- LinkedIn
- TikTok
- Stripe
- CRM platforms
- hosting
- domain providers
- email providers
- analytics tools

The Agency does not control platform outages, policy changes, account restrictions or third-party approval decisions.

## 16. Marketing results

The Agency does not guarantee:

- leads
- sales
- rankings
- follower growth
- ROAS
- revenue
- account approval
- advertising delivery

The Agency is responsible for the agreed professional work, not for results outside its reasonable control.

## 17. Regulated/high-risk content

The Client is responsible for notifying the Agency of regulatory restrictions.

The Agency may refuse to publish or advertise content it reasonably believes is unlawful, misleading or non-compliant.

## 18. Suspension

The Agency may suspend services after reasonable notice for:

- overdue payment
- missing required access
- serious security risk
- unlawful instruction
- material breach

Immediate suspension may be necessary for security, platform or legal risk.

## 19. Termination

Final termination rights require solicitor review.

The agreement should distinguish:

- end of fixed term
- one-month notice where contractually applicable
- termination for material breach
- insolvency
- unlawful conduct
- persistent non-payment

## 20. Effect of termination

On termination:

- final invoices remain payable
- access is removed/transferred appropriately
- Client-owned assets are made available as agreed
- confidential information remains protected
- outstanding third-party commitments remain the Client's responsibility where applicable

## 21. Liability

Final limitation and exclusion wording must be approved by a UK solicitor.

Do not exclude liability that cannot lawfully be excluded.

## 22. Force majeure

The final solicitor-approved agreement may include a reasonable clause for events outside a party's control.

## 23. Notices

Formal notices should be sent to the email/address stated in the Order Form.

## 24. Governing law

Final governing-law and jurisdiction provisions require solicitor approval.
