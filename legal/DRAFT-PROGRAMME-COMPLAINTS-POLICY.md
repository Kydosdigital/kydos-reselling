# DRAFT Programme Complaints Policy

Status: Draft for UK solicitor review before commercial launch.

Provider: KYDOS DIGITAL LTD

Support email: Support@kydosdigital.com

## 1. Purpose

Kydos wants programme participants to raise concerns early so they can be investigated and, where appropriate, corrected.

This policy covers complaints about:

- programme access
- implementation support
- done-for-you work
- staff conduct
- delivery delays
- billing administration
- programme materials
- handover
- support experience

It does not replace any statutory right or external dispute route available to the Participant.

## 2. Before making a formal complaint

Where the issue is a normal implementation question or small correction, contact the active Kydos support channel first.

If the issue is not resolved, or is serious, submit a formal complaint.

## 3. How to complain

Email:

Support@kydosdigital.com

Subject:

Formal Complaint, [Participant Name]

Include:

- full name
- programme tier
- purchase/start date
- clear description of the issue
- relevant dates
- supporting screenshots/files
- what outcome you are requesting

## 4. Acknowledgement

Kydos aims to acknowledge a formal complaint within 2 UK business days.

## 5. Investigation

Kydos will:

1. review the relevant agreement and Delivery Pack
2. review CRM, WhatsApp, email and file records where relevant
3. speak to involved staff
4. identify what was agreed
5. identify what was delivered
6. identify any outstanding work
7. decide whether remedial action is appropriate

## 6. Response target

Kydos aims to provide a substantive response within 10 UK business days.

More complex matters may take longer. If so, Kydos should explain the reason and provide an updated target.

## 7. Possible outcomes

Depending on the issue, Kydos may:

- explain the position
- correct an error
- complete outstanding contracted work
- replace/reassign a team member
- amend an implementation plan
- offer another reasonable remedy
- reject the complaint with reasons
- consider a refund or partial refund where legally or contractually appropriate

No staff member should promise compensation or a refund without authority.

## 8. Escalation

If the Participant remains dissatisfied, the complaint can be escalated to a director or senior Kydos decision-maker who was not the primary handler where practical.

## 9. Professional-service complaints

Complaints about an independent:

- accountant
- solicitor
- immigration adviser
- bank
- software provider
- recruiter
- contractor

must normally be raised with that provider under their own complaints process.

Kydos can help clarify the referral but does not control independent professional decisions.

## 10. Consumer rights

Nothing in this policy limits rights that cannot legally be excluded.

## 11. Records

Kydos should keep a complaint record containing:

- date
- issue
- owner
- evidence
- response
- outcome
- process improvement

## 12. Review

Review annually and whenever the Participant Agreement or Refund Policy materially changes.
