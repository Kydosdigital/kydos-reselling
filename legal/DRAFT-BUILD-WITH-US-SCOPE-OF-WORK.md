# DRAFT Build With Us Scope of Work

Programme tier:
£5,000 Build With Us

Status:
Draft for contract attachment.

## 1. Payment

Full payment before implementation begins.

## 2. Programme access

Includes access to the Blueprint materials and templates.

## 3. Active support

Priority implementation WhatsApp support for 12 weeks from the programme start date.

## 4. Company setup support

Kydos supports the Participant with:

- company-formation process
- ANNA route where appropriate
- Companies House process guidance
- business-bank setup guidance
- FreeAgent/accounting setup guidance
- accountant referral

Kydos does not guarantee:

- incorporation approval
- bank approval
- account opening time
- tax outcome

## 5. Brand creation

Participant supplies the required brief.

Kydos creates:

- logo
- colour palette
- typography direction
- basic brand guidelines

Included revision allowance for the programme-brand build should be stated in the final commercial order form.

Recommended:
2 consolidated revision rounds.

## 6. Website

One standard five-page agency website.

Participant may select any five standard pages.

Typical inclusions:

- responsive design
- enquiry/contact form
- booking integration
- basic on-page SEO
- CRM connection
- analytics/tracking readiness
- standard legal/footer links

Not included unless separately quoted:

- ecommerce
- custom application
- membership portal
- complex custom integrations
- more than five pages
- major bespoke development

## 7. CRM

Kydos configures:

- participant agency branding
- users
- sales pipeline
- fields
- tags
- booking calendar
- Stripe connection
- lead automations
- email/SMS/WhatsApp workflow where technically/provider supported
- e-signature workflow
- onboarding automation
- testing

Third-party/provider, messaging and usage charges are separate.

## 8. Sales infrastructure

Includes:

- sales scripts
- lead qualification
- objection handling
- follow-up sequence
- invoice/payment workflow
- CRM pipeline setup

## 9. Recruitment support

Kydos supports initial recruitment of:

- Account Manager
- Creative
- Sales Closer

The exact sourcing and candidate volume may depend on market availability.

Kydos does not guarantee future individual performance.

## 10. Client acquisition setup

Kydos helps set up the Participant's initial Meta lead-generation campaign.

Participant pays Meta ad spend.

Recommended initial ad budget:
£300.

No lead, client or revenue result is guaranteed.

## 11. Participant dependencies

Participant must provide:

- business information
- ID/verification required by third parties
- approvals
- payment methods
- domain/provider accounts
- requested access
- decisions
- ad budget

## 12. Target timeline

Target:
approximately 4 weeks for the core launch build where Participant responses and third-party processing allow.

This is not a guarantee that banks, Companies House, software providers or other third parties will complete within four weeks.

## 13. Completion

The scope is substantially complete when the agreed brand, website, CRM, core sales systems and agreed recruitment support have been delivered/tested and the remaining participant-operated actions are clearly handed over.
