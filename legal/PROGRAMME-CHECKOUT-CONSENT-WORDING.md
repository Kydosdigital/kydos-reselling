# Programme Checkout Consent Wording

Last reviewed:
31 August 2026

Status:
Draft for solicitor/checkout implementation review.

This is not a standalone legal policy. It is implementation wording for the checkout/contract process.

## Required purchase information

Before payment, show clearly:

- programme name/tier
- full price
- VAT
- what is included
- active support period
- third-party costs/exclusions
- implementation target
- cancellation/refund policy
- terms link
- privacy link

The payment button should make clear that the customer is placing an order with an obligation to pay.

## Checkbox 1, Terms

[ ] I have read and agree to the Programme Participant Agreement, Terms and Conditions and Refund and Cancellation Policy.

Do not pre-tick.

## Checkbox 2, Immediate digital content

Use where the purchaser is entitled to UK consumer cancellation rights and Kydos wants to release digital programme materials immediately.

[ ] I expressly request immediate access to the digital programme materials before the end of any applicable 14-day cancellation period. I understand that, once the digital content is supplied following my consent, I may lose the applicable right to cancel that digital-content supply.

Do not pre-tick.

Solicitor should confirm the exact Consumer Contracts Regulations wording and how the acknowledgement is recorded.

## Checkbox 3, Early start of services

Use where Kydos will begin implementation services during an applicable consumer cancellation period.

[ ] I expressly request Kydos Digital to begin the agreed implementation/services before the end of any applicable 14-day cancellation period. I understand that if I validly cancel after services have started, I may have to pay a proportionate amount for services already supplied, where the law allows.

Do not pre-tick.

## Confirmation email

After purchase, send a durable confirmation containing:

- customer name
- tier
- price
- date
- contract/terms
- cancellation information
- digital-content consent record
- service early-start request record
- support dates
- contact details

## Audit evidence

Store:

- timestamp
- checkbox version
- terms version
- IP/device/session information where proportionate
- confirmation email
- payment record
