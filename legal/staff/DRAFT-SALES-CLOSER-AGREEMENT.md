# DRAFT Sales Closer Agreement / Role Schedule

Status: Draft for legal/commercial review.

Pair with the correct underlying employment/contractor agreement after status assessment.

## Role

Sales Closer

## Duties

- respond quickly to fresh leads
- call/qualify prospects
- use CRM
- keep pipeline accurate
- conduct discovery calls
- recommend correct package
- handle objections
- follow up
- issue/trigger payment and agreement flow
- hand paid Clients to onboarding

## Speed-to-lead target

Fresh paid-media lead call:
within 60 seconds where operationally possible.

This is a performance target, not a guarantee that every lead will be reached in exactly 60 seconds.

## Commission

### Recurring services

50% of the first service fee actually received from a new Client, excluding VAT.

### One-off services

Draft Kydos working commission:
15% of the qualifying one-off service fee actually received, excluding VAT.

The final signed commercial schedule should list which services qualify and any exceptions.

## Commission conditions

Commission is calculated on cleared money received.

No commission on:

- VAT
- ad spend
- refunded/reversed amounts
- chargebacks
- fraudulent transactions
- unpaid invoices

The final agreement should state how later refunds/chargebacks affect commission already paid.

## No unauthorised promises

Sales Closer must not:

- guarantee results
- invent discounts
- change package scope
- promise unsupported delivery dates
- waive contracts
- misrepresent ad spend
- give legal/tax/immigration advice

## CRM records

Every active opportunity must include:

- stage
- notes
- next action
- next action date
- package
- outcome

## Confidentiality

Lead lists, scripts, prices and Client information are confidential.
