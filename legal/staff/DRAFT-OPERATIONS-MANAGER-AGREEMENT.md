# DRAFT Operations Manager Role Agreement / Schedule

Status: Draft.

Pair with the correct underlying employment/contractor agreement.

## Role purpose

Run day-to-day Agency delivery and report operational performance to the Agency Owner.

## Responsibilities

- manage Account Managers
- manage Creatives
- monitor deadlines
- review calendars
- oversee QA
- resolve blockers
- monitor Client health
- manage escalations
- review reports
- monitor capacity
- support probation/performance processes
- maintain operating trackers
- report to owner

## Authority

The Operations Manager may:

- reassign operational tasks
- request corrections
- escalate poor performance
- enforce documented SOPs
- coordinate Client recovery actions

Without express owner authority, the Operations Manager may not:

- change Client prices
- waive debt
- promise refunds
- sign major contracts
- hire/fire outside delegated authority
- admit legal liability

## KPIs

May include:

- on-time delivery
- overdue task count
- Client health
- repeated-error rate
- capacity
- reporting completion
- team performance
- escalations resolved

## Confidentiality

This role may access broad Client/staff information and has heightened security responsibilities.
