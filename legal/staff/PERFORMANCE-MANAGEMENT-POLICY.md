# Performance Management Policy

Status: Draft.

## Principle

Correct performance issues early and document them fairly.

## Informal stage

For a first/minor issue:

1. explain expectation
2. explain gap
3. agree correction
4. set review date
5. provide reasonable support

## Formal stage

Where issues are serious/repeated, use the applicable contractual/employee process.

Document:

- concern
- evidence
- expected standard
- required improvement
- support
- deadline
- consequence if not improved

## Examples

- repeated missed deadlines
- repeated Client complaints
- weak copy/design despite coaching
- inaccurate CRM records
- failure to follow security policy
- poor communication

## Not performance

Do not treat:

- disability-related issue
- protected leave
- lawful whistleblowing
- discrimination complaint
- other protected rights

as "poor performance".

Seek legal advice where employment rights are implicated.

## Contractors

For contractors, manage against:

- scope
- SLA
- quality
- deadlines

Use contractual remedies, not employee disciplinary language unless legally appropriate.
