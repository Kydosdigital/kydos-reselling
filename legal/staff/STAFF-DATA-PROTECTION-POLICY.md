# Staff Data Protection Policy

## Purpose

Staff and contractors handle personal data relating to:

- Clients
- leads
- candidates
- staff
- social followers
- website visitors

## Rules

Team members must:

- use approved systems
- access only assigned/required data
- keep accounts secure
- use MFA where available
- lock devices
- report incidents
- follow retention/deletion instructions
- avoid unnecessary downloads
- avoid copying Client data to personal cloud drives

## Personal devices

Where personal devices are allowed:

- use device passcode
- keep OS/software updated
- use secure connection
- separate work files where practical
- do not share device access
- delete Agency data on offboarding

## AI tools

Do not paste:

- personal data
- confidential Client strategy
- passwords
- unpublished sensitive documents

into unapproved AI tools.

Use anonymised/minimised information where AI assistance is permitted.

## WhatsApp

Do not use ordinary WhatsApp messages as the permanent record for:

- passwords
- special-category data
- banking credentials
- large Client datasets

## Incidents

Immediately report:

- wrong-recipient email
- lost device
- compromised password
- suspicious login
- accidental public file
- Client data shared with wrong person
- malware

## Offboarding

Access must be removed promptly.

Staff must return/delete Agency and Client data as instructed.

## Monitoring

The Agency may monitor business systems where lawful, proportionate and disclosed.

## Breach

Serious or repeated breach may lead to disciplinary/contractual action subject to applicable law.
