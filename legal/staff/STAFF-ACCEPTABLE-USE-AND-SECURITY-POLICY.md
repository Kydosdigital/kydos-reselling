# Staff Acceptable Use and Security Policy

## Accounts

- use your own assigned account
- never share MFA
- never reuse the Agency password elsewhere
- use approved password manager
- remove old sessions/devices

## Client systems

Only access Client systems needed for your role.

Do not:

- change billing
- add administrators
- export contact lists
- delete campaigns/data
- alter ownership

without authority.

## Software

Use approved/licensed software.

Do not install cracked/pirated tools.

## Files

Store work in approved Agency/Client storage.

Do not keep the only copy on a personal laptop.

## Email

Check recipient before sending.

Treat unexpected attachments/links as suspicious.

## Social media

Do not post on a Client account unless:

- assigned
- content approved
- correct account confirmed

## Banking/payments

Only authorised people access banking/Stripe administration.

No staff member should ask the owner to send banking password or MFA code over chat.

## Remote work

Use:

- secure device
- private connection
- appropriate screen privacy

Avoid confidential work on open public Wi-Fi without suitable protection.

## AI and automation

Use only in accordance with Client and Agency policy.

Human review remains required for Client-facing work.

## Incident reporting

Report immediately to Operations Manager/Owner.

Do not hide a mistake because you are worried about consequences.
