# DRAFT Creative Role Agreement / Schedule

Status: Draft.

Pair with the correct underlying Employment Agreement or Independent Contractor Services Agreement after status assessment.

## Role

Creative

Reports to:
Operations Manager, receiving delivery briefs from Account Managers.

## Core duties

- static graphics
- carousels
- Story design
- video/reel editing
- subtitles
- correct exports
- source-file organisation
- brand consistency
- Client corrections

## Capacity

Normal:
4 Client accounts.

Upper working capacity:
5.

## Weekly deadline

Standard weekly Client batch:
Monday, 1:00 PM UK time.

Late/missing briefs or assets must be raised early.

## Quality

Creative must check:

- spelling
- logo
- fonts
- colours
- dimensions
- mobile readability
- asset quality
- licences/permissions
- subtitles
- Client rules

## Tools

May include:

- Adobe tools
- CapCut
- Canva
- Freepik
- Envato

The Company may change tools.

## AI visual use

Do not substitute AI-generated imagery for real/client imagery where authenticity matters unless approved.

Follow Client and Agency rules.

## Intellectual property

Final deliverables/source files created for the Agency are governed by the signed employment/contractor IP terms.

Do not reuse confidential Client creative for unrelated businesses without permission.

## Performance indicators

- deadline compliance
- quality
- correction rate
- file organisation
- brand adherence
- communication
- Client/AM feedback

## Compensation

Set in underlying agreement.
