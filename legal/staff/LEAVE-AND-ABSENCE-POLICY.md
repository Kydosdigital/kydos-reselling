# Leave and Absence Policy

Status: Draft. Employee rights depend on jurisdiction and must be legally reviewed.

## Employees

Employees receive at least applicable statutory leave rights.

The Employment Agreement should state:

- annual leave entitlement
- leave year
- booking process
- carry-over rules
- sick absence process
- statutory family leave rights where applicable

## Contractors

Genuine contractors do not automatically receive employee holiday/sick-pay benefits unless law or contract provides otherwise.

Do not use this policy to disguise an employee as a contractor.

## Planned leave

Request as early as possible.

Manager should check:

- Client deadlines
- handover
- coverage
- approvals
- scheduled campaigns

## Handover

Before leave:

- update task system
- flag urgent Client items
- hand over approvals
- identify backup
- schedule content where possible

## Sickness/emergency

Notify manager promptly.

Provide legally permitted evidence where required by the applicable employment arrangement.

## Unauthorised absence

Repeated failure to communicate absence may become a performance/disciplinary matter, subject to applicable law.
