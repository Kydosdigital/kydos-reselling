# Working Hours and Availability Policy

Status: Operational draft. Employment-law/local-law review required for employees.

## Purpose

Set predictable working coverage without misclassifying contractors or breaching local working-time laws.

## Employees

Working hours must be stated in the Employment Agreement and comply with applicable law.

Kydos-style delivery operates around UK deadlines.

## Contractors

For genuine independent contractors, focus on:

- deliverables
- deadlines
- agreed communication windows

Do not impose employee-style control if the relationship is intended to be genuinely independent.

## Core operational deadlines

- monthly content calendars: internally by the 25th
- weekly Creative batch: Monday, 1:00 PM UK time

## Availability

Team members must communicate planned unavailability early.

For urgent unexpected absence:
notify manager as soon as reasonably possible.

## Client messages

Account Managers should check Client communication during their agreed working window and not leave urgent service issues unattended.

## Out-of-hours

The Agency does not require 24/7 work unless a specific role/service expressly includes it.

## Time zones

Overseas team schedules must be agreed clearly so UK deadlines remain realistic.

## Overtime

Employee overtime/working-time rules must be handled according to the applicable employment law and contract.

Contractors should not be managed through unpaid "overtime" language inconsistent with genuine contracting.
