## 1. Services

## 2. Independent business

The parties intend an independent contractor relationship where legally supported by the facts.

The Contractor is responsible for their own business taxes, registrations and insurance except where law requires otherwise.

This clause does not override statutory employment status.

## 8. Confidentiality

The Contractor must protect Client and Agency confidential information.

## 9. Data protection/security

Contractor must follow the Staff Data Protection and Acceptable Use/Security policies where applicable.

## 10. Intellectual property

Subject to local law, the Contractor assigns to the Company the agreed rights in bespoke work created and paid for under this engagement, excluding pre-existing contractor tools/background IP identified in writing.

The final assignment wording requires legal review for the relevant jurisdiction.

## 11. Client contact

The Contractor must not:

- bind the Company to new commercial terms
- offer refunds
- disclose confidential information
- solicit Client business outside the Agency in breach of agreed restrictions

Any non-solicitation restriction must be reasonable and legally reviewed.

## 12. Quality and correction

Contractor will correct work that materially fails the agreed brief/standard within a reasonable time.
