# DRAFT Account Manager Role Agreement / Schedule

Status: Draft.

This Role Schedule must be paired with the correct underlying Employment Agreement or Independent Contractor Services Agreement after an employment-status assessment.

## Role

Account Manager

Reports to:
Operations Manager or Agency Owner

## Core duties

- manage assigned Client accounts
- research Client businesses and competitors
- create monthly content calendars
- write captions, hooks, CTAs and social copy
- maintain Client Content Rules Sheets
- brief Creatives
- review Creative output
- manage approvals
- schedule/publish approved content
- perform community management where included
- prepare monthly reports
- escalate risks

## Capacity

Normal:
4 Client accounts.

Upper working capacity:
5 Client accounts.

Additional capacity requires management approval.

## Key deadlines

- following month's calendar due internally by the 25th
- weekly Creative brief issued early enough for Monday 1:00 PM UK Creative deadline
- Client messages/actions handled within Agency expectations

## Quality standard

Account Manager must:

- research before writing
- avoid generic content
- follow Client rules
- check facts/claims
- use British spelling for UK Clients where appropriate
- maintain professional communication
- record corrections

## AI use

AI may support research, ideation and proofreading.

The Account Manager remains responsible for final accuracy, tone and quality.

## Confidentiality/data

The Account Manager may access Client confidential information and must follow all security/data policies.

## Performance indicators

May include:

- deadline compliance
- Client satisfaction
- calendar quality
- copy quality
- repeated-error rate
- reporting
- approval management
- Client retention indicators

## Compensation

Set in the underlying agreement.

Kydos internal salary budgets are not published in the programme.

## Probation/review

If employee:
3-month draft probation subject to final/local-law terms.

If contractor:
use an initial service review period rather than falsely labelling it statutory employment probation.
