# Probation Policy

Status: Draft for employment-law review.

## Employees

Kydos draft standard:

Probation:
3 months.

Draft notice during probation:
14 days.

Draft notice after probation:
30 days.

The employment agreement and local law control.

## Purpose

Probation is used to assess:

- role competence
- reliability
- quality
- communication
- process compliance
- team fit
- Client handling where relevant

## Review points

Suggested:

- 30 days
- 60 days
- end of probation

## Outcomes

- pass
- continue with improvement plan
- extend where contract/law permits
- terminate in accordance with contract/law

## Evidence

Use:

- deadlines
- work quality
- Client feedback
- manager notes
- repeated errors
- training completed

## Contractors

Do not call a contractor review period "employment probation" if the relationship is genuinely self-employed.

Use:
Initial Service Review Period

and define deliverables/termination in the contractor agreement.
