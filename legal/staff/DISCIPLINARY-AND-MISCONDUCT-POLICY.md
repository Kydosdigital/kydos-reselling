# Disciplinary and Misconduct Policy

Status: Draft for UK/local employment-law review.

For UK employees, the final process should be aligned with applicable law and the ACAS Code.

## Examples of misconduct

May include:

- repeated unauthorised absence
- deliberate failure to follow reasonable procedures
- inappropriate Client communication
- misuse of systems
- repeated security failures

## Potential gross misconduct

Depending on facts/law:

- fraud
- theft
- serious confidentiality breach
- deliberate data theft
- serious harassment
- deliberate Client sabotage
- serious unauthorised account access
- violence/threats

Do not automatically label conduct gross misconduct without investigation.

## Fair process

For employees, a reasonable process may include:

1. investigation
2. written allegation
3. opportunity to respond
4. meeting
5. decision
6. appeal

## Contractors

Use the Contractor Agreement's breach/termination provisions rather than automatically applying employee disciplinary rights/procedures.

## Records

Keep secure records and restrict access.
