# DRAFT Staff Confidentiality and Intellectual Property Agreement

Status: Draft for jurisdiction-specific legal review.

## 1. Confidential information

Includes non-public:

- Client information
- lead lists
- pricing
- strategies
- passwords/access information
- CRM data
- staff records
- financial information
- SOPs
- proposals
- campaign data
- unpublished creative
- business plans

## 2. Obligations

Team member must:

- use confidential information only for authorised work
- keep it secure
- not send it to personal/unapproved systems
- not disclose it to third parties
- report loss/disclosure promptly
- return/delete it when instructed

## 3. Exclusions

Confidential information does not normally include information that can be shown to be:

- lawfully public
- already lawfully known
- independently developed
- lawfully received without confidentiality duty

## 4. Client confidentiality

Client data must not be:

- used in portfolios without permission
- shared on social media
- uploaded to public AI/tools contrary to policy
- used for unrelated clients

## 5. Intellectual property

The correct ownership/assignment depends on whether the person is an employee or contractor and applicable jurisdiction.

### Employee

Work created in the course of employment will be handled under applicable employment/IP law and the employment agreement.

### Contractor

The contractor agreement should contain an express written assignment/licence for agreed bespoke deliverables.

Do not assume contractor IP automatically belongs to the Agency.

## 6. Moral rights

Any waiver/consent should be reviewed for enforceability in the relevant jurisdiction.

## 7. Pre-existing IP

Team member should identify pre-existing tools/assets they intend to use.

## 8. Third-party IP

Do not use unlicensed:

- fonts
- images
- music
- stock
- templates
- code

## 9. Survival

Confidentiality obligations continue after the engagement where legally enforceable.

## 10. Remedies

Final remedies clause requires solicitor review.
