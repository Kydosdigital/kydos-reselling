## 2. Information we may collect

Programme enquiries and participants:

- name
- email
- phone
- company/business name
- billing details
- programme tier
- support messages
- implementation notes
- CRM records
- call/meeting notes
- files supplied for implementation
- website/domain/business setup information
- payment status

Recruitment-related data may be handled under a separate Recruitment Privacy Notice.

## 3. Why we use information

We may use personal data to:

- respond to enquiries
- enter and perform the programme contract
- provide support
- create participant accounts
- deliver implementation work
- process payments and invoices
- manage complaints
- maintain security
- keep accounting/business records
- improve programme operations
- comply with legal obligations
- communicate relevant service information

## 4. Lawful bases

Depending on the activity, Kydos may rely on:

- contract
- steps taken before entering a contract
- legal obligation
- legitimate interests
- consent where required

Marketing consent and cookie consent should be handled separately where applicable.

## 5. Special category data

Kydos does not need participants to provide sensitive personal information for ordinary programme delivery.

If immigration circumstances or other sensitive matters arise, participants should share only what is necessary with the appropriate regulated professional.

Kydos should avoid storing unnecessary sensitive immigration/health information.

## 6. Who we may share information with

Where required:

- CRM/software providers
- cloud storage providers
- payment processors
- accountant
- solicitor
- immigration adviser, only with appropriate basis/permission
- recruitment providers
- website/technical contractors
- communication providers
- regulators/law enforcement where legally required

Each provider should receive only the information needed for its role.

## 7. International processing

Some technology providers or contractors may process data outside the UK.

Kydos should use appropriate contractual and legal safeguards where required.

## 8. Retention

Kydos should keep personal information only for as long as needed for:

- programme delivery
- support
- accounting/tax records
- legal claims
- security
- statutory obligations

The final published notice should include or link to Kydos's retention schedule.

## 9. Rights

Depending on the circumstances, individuals may have rights including:

- access
- correction
- erasure
- restriction
- objection
- portability
- withdrawal of consent

Rights are not absolute in every situation.

## 10. Complaints

Contact Kydos first if you have a privacy concern.

Individuals can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

## 11. Security

Kydos uses access controls, role-based permissions and secure systems appropriate to the data handled.

Do not send banking passwords, primary email passwords or MFA codes through ordinary support messages.

## 12. Cookies and website tracking

Website cookies and tracking are covered by the website Cookie Notice and consent tool.

Non-essential cookies should not be set before valid consent where consent is required.

## 13. Changes

The notice may be updated when systems, providers or legal requirements change.
