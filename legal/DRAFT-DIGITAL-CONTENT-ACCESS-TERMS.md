# DRAFT Digital Content Access Terms

Status: Draft for solicitor review.

These terms supplement the Programme Participant Agreement where the Participant receives digital programme materials.

## 1. Digital content

Digital content may include:

- written guides
- templates
- spreadsheets
- calculators
- videos
- portal modules
- checklists
- scripts
- downloadable files

## 2. Access start

Unless stated otherwise, access begins after:

- full payment
- completion of required contract steps
- required consumer consent/acknowledgement where applicable

## 3. Lifetime access

Kydos's commercial intention is lifetime access to the purchased programme materials.

For final legal wording, "lifetime" should be defined as access for as long as Kydos continues to operate/provide the programme or substantially equivalent access, subject to lawful termination, platform changes and events outside reasonable control.

Kydos may:

- migrate platform
- replace file formats
- consolidate modules
- update outdated guidance
- remove unlawful/infringing material

while preserving substantially equivalent access where reasonably possible.

## 4. Immediate access and consumer cancellation rights

Where the Participant is a consumer and UK cancellation rights apply, Kydos will use an appropriate checkout/contract process before immediate digital supply.

The process should obtain:

- express agreement to immediate supply
- acknowledgement of the effect this has on the applicable right to cancel once supply begins, where UK law provides

Kydos should send confirmation in a durable medium such as email.

## 5. Quality

Digital content must match its description and any non-excludable statutory quality requirements.

If a file is corrupted or inaccessible, Kydos should provide a replacement or reasonable access remedy.

## 6. Updates

Time-sensitive business, tax, platform and immigration information may be updated.

Participants must check current official rules before acting on regulated or time-sensitive matters.

## 7. Use rights

Template use is governed by the Template Use Licence.

## 8. Security

Participants must:

- keep login details secure
- not compromise the portal
- not share Kydos confidential admin access
- report suspected account compromise

## 9. Third-party platform

Portal hosting may depend on a third-party platform.

Kydos may move the content to another platform if needed.

## 10. End of active support

Expiry of WhatsApp/implementation support does not automatically end access to digital materials.
