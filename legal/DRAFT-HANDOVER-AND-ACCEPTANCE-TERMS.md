# DRAFT Handover and Acceptance Terms

Status: Draft for solicitor review.

These terms apply primarily to Build With Us and Done For You implementation work.

## 1. Handover

Kydos will provide a handover record identifying:

- completed items
- participant-owned accounts
- access status
- outstanding dependencies
- known defects/issues
- training completed
- support period

## 2. Participant review

The Participant should review handover items promptly.

Recommended review period:
5 UK business days.

During that period, the Participant should identify:

- missing agreed item
- access issue
- material functional defect
- incorrect business information

## 3. Acceptance

Acceptance may occur when:

- the Participant signs the handover record
- the Participant confirms acceptance in writing
- the Participant begins using the delivered system in live operation without reporting a material handover defect within the review period

The final deemed-acceptance wording must be reviewed for fairness, especially for consumers.

## 4. Corrections

Kydos will correct genuine defects within the agreed scope.

A defect is not:

- a new design preference
- new page
- new automation
- new feature
- new employee request
- new business strategy
- third-party outage
- change in provider policy

Those may be separately quoted.

## 5. Outstanding third-party items

A handover can record an external dependency such as:

- bank approval
- domain transfer
- software verification
- recruitment candidate start date
- solicitor/accountant action

Such an item should have:

- owner
- status
- next action

## 6. Access security

Passwords must not be written in the handover document.

Use secure credential transfer.

## 7. Support start

For Done For You, the 90-day post-handover support period begins on the formal handover date stated in the signed handover record.

## 8. Ownership

Participant-owned brand assets, website deliverables and configured systems are handed over according to the applicable contract and third-party platform terms.

Kydos retains its pre-existing templates, know-how, SOP library and other background IP except for rights licensed to the Participant.
