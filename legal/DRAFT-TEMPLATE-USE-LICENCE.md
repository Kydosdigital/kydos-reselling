# DRAFT Template Use Licence

Status: Draft for solicitor review.

This licence applies to templates supplied as part of the Kydos Digital agency programme.

## 1. Licence grant

Subject to full payment and compliance with the Programme Agreement, Kydos grants the Participant a broad commercial licence to:

- copy
- edit
- adapt
- rebrand
- use with their own clients
- use internally
- include adapted versions in their own service delivery
- teach from adapted versions
- redistribute adapted templates
- resell adapted templates

This reflects Kydos's commercial intention to give programme participants practical freedom to build their own agency assets.

## 2. What is not transferred

The Participant does not acquire ownership of:

- the Kydos Digital name
- Kydos logos
- Kydos trade marks
- Kydos confidential information
- real Kydos client data
- personal data
- third-party intellectual property
- licensed stock assets
- third-party software
- proprietary platform code

## 3. Kydos worked examples

Worked examples may be used for learning.

Before redistribution or resale, the Participant must remove:

- Kydos branding unless permission is given
- confidential information
- identifiable client information
- personal data
- third-party material not licensed for redistribution

## 4. Client case studies

Where case studies are anonymised for training, the underlying original client identity and source data remain confidential.

Participants must not attempt to identify, publish or reverse-engineer confidential client information.

## 5. Legal templates

Legal templates are educational starting points.

The Participant must not market them as:

- legal advice from Kydos
- solicitor-approved for every jurisdiction
- guaranteed legally compliant for every client

Participants should obtain legal review for their own business where appropriate.

## 6. No Kydos endorsement

Using or reselling a template does not mean Kydos endorses the Participant's business, service, legal position or modified document.

## 7. Third-party material

Where a template includes or references third-party material, that material remains subject to the third party's licence.

This licence does not give rights Kydos does not own.

## 8. Termination

If the Programme Agreement is lawfully terminated for serious misuse, fraud, unlawful conduct or non-payment/reversal, Kydos may terminate future use rights to confidential or proprietary programme material to the extent permitted by law.

The final clause should be reviewed for fairness and enforceability.

## 9. Survival

Rights validly granted to templates already lawfully received should be handled according to the final solicitor-approved agreement.

## 10. Brand protection

The Participant must not:

- register Kydos trade marks
- impersonate Kydos
- claim to be a Kydos branch/franchise unless separately authorised
- use Kydos branding in a misleading way

Solicitor review required before use.
