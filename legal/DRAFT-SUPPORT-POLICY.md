# DRAFT Programme Support Policy

## Support channels

Primary:
WhatsApp

Secondary where required:
Email

Kydos may later provide support inside the CRM/community portal.

## Support periods

### Blueprint

8 weeks from programme start date.

### Build With Us

12 weeks from programme start date.

### Done For You

Active support during implementation plus 90 days from formal handover.

## Support level

### Standard

Blueprint.

Includes:
- clarification
- navigation
- implementation questions
- template questions
- reasonable troubleshooting

### Priority implementation

Build With Us.

Includes standard support plus questions relating to the systems Kydos is actively helping to build.

### Priority handover

Done For You.

Includes support for the systems and initial team established under the Done For You scope.

## What support does not mean

Support is not:

- unlimited consulting
- unlimited copywriting
- unlimited campaign management
- unlimited website changes
- unlimited recruitment
- 24/7 emergency coverage
- legal advice
- tax advice
- immigration advice

## Response target

Draft service target:

Kydos aims to respond within one UK business day during the active support period.

Complex questions may take longer.

This is a target, not a 24/7 guarantee.

## Business hours

Draft support window:

Monday to Friday, UK business days.

Public holidays and planned closures may affect response time.

## WhatsApp etiquette

Participants should:

- keep questions reasonably organised
- avoid repeated messages across multiple numbers
- provide screenshots/context
- allow time for investigation
- use the emergency/escalation route only for genuine urgent implementation blockers

## After support period

Participants retain programme-material access according to their access terms.

Further personal implementation support, consulting or done-for-you work may be purchased separately.

Final wording should be checked against the Participant Agreement.
