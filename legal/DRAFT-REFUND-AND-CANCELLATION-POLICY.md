# DRAFT Refund and Cancellation Policy

Status:
Draft for UK solicitor review before publication.

## Kydos commercial position

Programme fees are paid in full.

Because access may include substantial digital materials and implementation may begin quickly, Kydos does not offer a discretionary "change of mind at any time" refund scheme.

However, this policy does not remove statutory consumer rights.

A blanket "all payments are non-refundable in every circumstance" rule should not be used.

## 1. If the purchaser is acting as a consumer

Where UK distance-selling cancellation rights apply, the Participant may have a statutory cancellation period.

The exact rights depend on whether the contract includes:

- digital content
- services
- bespoke/custom work
- a combination

## 2. Immediate digital-content access

Where Kydos supplies digital content immediately during an applicable cancellation period, the purchase flow must obtain:

1. express consent to begin supply immediately
2. acknowledgement that the applicable right to cancel the digital-content supply will be lost once supply begins, where the law provides for this

The confirmation of contract should record this.

If Kydos fails to obtain the required consent/acknowledgement, it should not assume the statutory cancellation right has been lost.

## 3. Services starting during a cancellation period

Where a consumer asks Kydos to start services before the end of an applicable cancellation period:

- Kydos should obtain the required express request
- if the consumer later validly cancels before full performance, the consumer may be required to pay a proportionate amount for services already supplied, where the law permits
- if services are fully performed following the legally required request/acknowledgement, cancellation rights may be affected

Final wording must be checked by solicitor.

## 4. After statutory cancellation rights expire or are validly lost

Refund requests will be assessed against:

- selected programme tier
- materials already supplied
- work already performed
- bespoke assets already created
- recruitment already undertaken
- third-party costs incurred
- contractual breach, if any
- statutory rights

Kydos is not required to provide a goodwill refund simply because the Participant:

- changes business idea
- decides not to launch
- does not read the materials
- does not attend/support implementation
- fails to provide information
- fails to use the systems
- does not acquire clients
- changes immigration plans

This is subject to applicable law and fairness.

## 5. Kydos failure to deliver

If Kydos materially fails to provide contracted services, the Participant should first use the complaints process.

Any remedy/refund should reflect:
- the legal rights applicable
- work properly delivered
- undelivered scope
- opportunity to remedy where appropriate

## 6. Third-party fees

Refunds from third parties such as:

- banks
- software providers
- domain providers
- advertising platforms
- accountants
- solicitors
- recruiters

are governed by those providers' own terms.

Kydos cannot promise third-party refunds.

## 7. How to request cancellation/refund

Email:

Support@kydosdigital.com

Include:

- full name
- programme tier
- purchase date
- reason
- requested outcome

Kydos will acknowledge and review the request.

## 8. Statutory rights

Nothing in this policy excludes rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

## Reference point

UK government online/distance selling guidance explains the 14-day framework and the specific consent requirement for immediate digital content.

The final live policy must be reviewed by Kydos's UK solicitor before customer use.
