## Information collected

During recruitment we may collect:

- name
- contact details
- CV
- work history
- education
- portfolio
- interview notes
- test-task results
- references
- right-to-work/identity information where required
- compensation expectations
- availability

We should not collect unnecessary sensitive information.

## Purpose

We use candidate information to:

- manage applications
- assess suitability
- conduct interviews
- verify information
- make offers
- keep recruitment records
- comply with legal obligations

## Lawful basis

The lawful basis depends on the activity and should be recorded.

Possible bases can include:

- steps at the candidate's request before entering a contract
- legitimate interests
- legal obligation
- consent for optional talent-pool retention where appropriate

Special-category/criminal-offence data needs additional legal conditions where processed.

## Sharing

Information may be shared with:

- hiring managers
- authorised recruiters
- background/reference providers
- legal/accounting advisers where necessary
- IT/cloud providers

## Automated tools/AI

If AI is used to assist screening, Kydos must ensure use is fair, transparent and appropriately reviewed.

Do not rely on opaque automated rejection without checking data-protection/employment implications.

## Retention

Draft Kydos standard:

Unsuccessful candidate records:
up to 6 months after the recruitment decision unless a longer period is justified or the candidate agrees to a talent pool.

Successful candidate data:
moves to the staff record and follows the staff-retention schedule.

Final periods should be approved and documented.

## Rights

Candidates may have rights under UK GDPR including access, correction, objection and erasure depending on circumstances.

## Complaints

Contact the Agency first.

Candidates may also complain to the ICO.

Official ICO recruitment guidance:

https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/employment/recruitment-and-selection/
