# DRAFT Done For You Scope of Work

Programme tier:
£10,000 Done For You Agency

Status:
Draft for contract attachment.

## 1. Payment

Full payment before implementation begins.

## 2. Programme access

Includes access to the full programme library within the purchased tier.

## 3. Company foundation

Kydos coordinates the agreed setup process for:

- company formation
- banking guidance
- accounting setup coordination
- accountant referral
- domain
- business email
- internal file structure

Professional fees and third-party charges are separate unless expressly stated in the order form.

## 4. Brand

Kydos creates:

- logo
- colour palette
- typography
- basic brand guidelines

## 5. Website

One standard five-page agency website.

Additional pages or custom functionality require separate scope.

## 6. CRM

Kydos configures and tests:

- branded CRM
- users
- pipeline
- forms
- booking
- email
- SMS
- WhatsApp where technically/provider supported
- Stripe
- invoice/payment process
- e-signature
- onboarding automation
- lead notifications

## 7. Initial team recruitment

Kydos recruits/sources the agreed launch team:

- Account Manager
- Creative
- Sales Closer
- Operations Manager

Kydos may use third-party recruitment sources.

Employment/contractor classification and jurisdiction-specific engagement terms must be checked before final appointment.

## 8. Staff training

Kydos provides role onboarding for the initial launch team on:

- manuals
- SOPs
- task structure
- deadlines
- quality control
- client onboarding
- CRM/sales handover
- escalation
- reporting

## 9. Operations setup

Includes:

- client folder template
- task-management structure
- capacity trackers
- client health process
- QA checklist
- management dashboard specification
- Operations Manager routines
- retention/renewal system

## 10. Marketing launch

Kydos prepares the initial Meta lead-generation launch system.

Participant pays ad spend.

Recommended starting Meta budget:
£300.

No result is guaranteed.

## 11. Handover

Kydos completes the Done For You Handover Pack.

The Participant receives:

- business-system records
- brand assets
- website access
- CRM access
- SOP library
- templates
- initial team details
- operational trackers
- secure access handover

## 12. Active support

Priority implementation support during the build plus 90 days after formal handover.

## 13. Participant role after handover

The Participant owns and oversees the agency.

Kydos does not become the permanent director, employer, manager or operator of the Participant's company.

## 14. Dependencies

Timeline depends on:

- Participant decisions
- third-party onboarding
- candidate availability
- professional-adviser turnaround
- platform approvals
- bank/company processing
- access provision

## 15. Completion

The Done For You scope is complete when:

- agreed infrastructure is delivered
- agreed initial team recruitment/training is completed or documented as an agreed outstanding dependency
- handover pack is completed
- support period begins
