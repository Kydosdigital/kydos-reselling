# Social Media Management SOP

This is the end-to-end monthly delivery process.

## 1. Onboarding

Complete:
- payment
- agreement
- onboarding form
- Drive
- assets
- access
- Account Manager assignment
- Creative assignment
- Content Rules Sheet

## 2. Monthly research

Account Manager reviews:
- previous month
- business priorities
- upcoming dates
- offers
- competitors
- audience
- platform trends
- client corrections

## 3. Calendar creation

Create the following month's calendar by the 25th.

Calendar includes:
- date/week
- platform
- format
- content pillar
- concept
- hook
- caption
- CTA
- hashtags/keywords
- visual direction

## 4. Internal review

Operations Manager or CEO reviews before client.

Check:
- strategy
- repetition
- accuracy
- compliance
- brand fit
- quality
- commercial relevance

## 5. Client approval

Send calendar.

Target response:
3 days.

Included:
2 revision rounds.

Silence is not approval.

## 6. Weekly creative brief

After approval, Account Manager creates the weekly brief.

## 7. Creative production

Creative produces:
- graphics
- carousels
- reels/videos
- story assets

Deadline:
Monday, 1:00 PM UK time.

## 8. Internal creative review

Account Manager reviews the actual creative.

Escalate material quality concerns to Operations Manager.

## 9. Client creative approval

Send content to client.

Record:
- approved
- changes requested
- pending

## 10. Schedule/publish

Only approved content is published.

Use:
- CRM scheduler
- native tools where needed

## 11. Stories

Deliver according to package.

Story publishing is owned by Account Manager.

Story design is owned by Creative where design is required.

## 12. Community management

Deliver according to package.

Respond professionally and escalate sensitive matters.

## 13. Reactive content

A timely post may replace a scheduled item.

Do not automatically increase the monthly content count.

## 14. Monthly reporting

Prepare report at month end.

Include:
- output delivered
- growth
- reach/impressions
- engagement
- top content
- audience insights where available
- enquiries/conversions where trackable
- recommendations
- next-month actions

## 15. Renewal/retention

Watch for:
- repeated approval delays
- dissatisfaction
- poor response
- payment issues
- falling engagement
- out-of-scope requests

Escalate churn risk early.
