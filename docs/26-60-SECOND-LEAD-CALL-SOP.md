# 60-Second Lead Call SOP

Kydos's sales model prioritises speed.

The target is for the Sales Closer to call a fresh paid-media lead within 60 seconds of the CRM notification where operationally possible.

This is a target, not a promise that every lead will always be reached within exactly 60 seconds.

## Why speed matters

A fresh lead:
- remembers the advert
- still has the problem in mind
- is more likely to answer
- may be enquiring with competitors at the same time

## Workflow

1. Lead enters CRM.
2. CRM sends email, SMS and WhatsApp confirmation.
3. Sales Closer receives alert.
4. Open contact record.
5. Check:
   - name
   - company
   - service interest
   - enquiry source
6. Call immediately.
7. Record outcome.
8. Move pipeline stage.
9. Set next action.

## If they answer

Opening:

"Hi [Name], it's [Salesperson] from [Agency]. You just sent us an enquiry about [service], so I wanted to come straight back to you while it was fresh. Have you got two minutes?"

Then qualify.

## If they do not answer

- leave a short voicemail where appropriate
- send a short follow-up
- record attempt
- schedule next attempt
- do not repeatedly call in a way that becomes intrusive

## Required CRM note

After every call record:
- answered/not answered
- need
- current marketing
- budget
- decision-maker
- urgency
- objection
- next step
- next contact date
