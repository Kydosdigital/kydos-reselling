# Accountant Onboarding Checklist

Use this when appointing an accountant for the agency.

Kydos can refer programme participants to the accountant used by Kydos where appropriate.

## Before engagement

Ask:
- [ ] Do you regularly work with UK limited companies?
- [ ] Do you support service businesses and agencies?
- [ ] Do you work with FreeAgent?
- [ ] Do you provide annual accounts?
- [ ] Do you provide Corporation Tax returns?
- [ ] Do you provide VAT support?
- [ ] Do you provide payroll/PAYE support?
- [ ] Do you advise on director remuneration?
- [ ] Do you provide bookkeeping or only year-end accounts?
- [ ] How do you communicate with clients?
- [ ] What is included in the monthly fee?
- [ ] What costs extra?
- [ ] What records do you need each month?

## Information to provide

- [ ] Certificate of incorporation
- [ ] Company number
- [ ] Registered office
- [ ] Director details
- [ ] Shareholder details
- [ ] Accounting reference date
- [ ] Bank details
- [ ] FreeAgent access
- [ ] VAT status
- [ ] PAYE status
- [ ] Existing invoices
- [ ] Existing expenses and receipts
- [ ] Previous accounts if applicable
- [ ] HMRC correspondence
- [ ] Companies House correspondence
- [ ] Expected turnover
- [ ] Staff and contractor structure

## Access

Invite the accountant through FreeAgent.

Do not share:
- banking passwords
- personal authentication codes
- personal email passwords

## Agree responsibility

Write down who is responsible for:
- bookkeeping
- bank reconciliation
- payroll
- VAT returns
- Corporation Tax return
- annual accounts
- confirmation statement
- Companies House changes
- director tax return if required
- reminders
- responding to HMRC

## Monthly owner check-in

The owner should still review:
- sales
- cash
- profit
- unpaid invoices
- VAT position
- tax reserve
- payroll cost
- major expenses
- filing deadlines

"Ask the accountant" is not a substitute for understanding whether the company is financially healthy.
