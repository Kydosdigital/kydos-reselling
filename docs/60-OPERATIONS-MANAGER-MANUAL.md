# Operations Manager Manual

The Operations Manager runs the day-to-day delivery operation so the owner does not have to personally manage every task.

## Core outcome

The Operations Manager should make sure:

- every client has a clear owner
- every deadline is visible
- every team member knows what is due
- weak work is corrected before the client sees it
- client issues are escalated early
- workload is balanced
- monthly reporting happens
- recurring mistakes are turned into process improvements
- the owner receives a concise operational summary instead of being dragged into every small issue

## Reporting line

Reports to:
Agency Owner

Manages operationally:
- Account Managers
- Creatives
- Sales handover issues
- specialist contractors where delivery overlaps with client work

## Daily responsibilities

1. Review urgent client issues.
2. Check overdue tasks.
3. Check work due in the next 48 hours.
4. Check client approvals that are blocking delivery.
5. Check Creative workload.
6. Check Account Manager workload.
7. Resolve blockers.
8. Review work that needs management-level QA.
9. Escalate serious commercial, compliance or reputational issues.
10. Update owner where a decision is required.

## Weekly responsibilities

- capacity review
- client status review
- missed-deadline review
- approval bottleneck review
- quality review
- staff issue review
- next-week risk review
- sales-to-delivery handover review
- payment-risk awareness
- owner summary

## Monthly responsibilities

- confirm next month's calendars are due internally by the 25th
- confirm monthly reports are produced
- review client health scores
- review churn risks
- review workload
- review staff performance
- identify repeated quality issues
- recommend training
- recommend hiring where capacity is exceeded
- review process failures
- review recurring client complaints
- report major operational lessons to owner

## Quality-control role

The Operations Manager does not need to personally rewrite every caption or redesign every graphic.

The job is to ensure the process catches weak work.

Management QA should focus on:

- strategic relevance
- factual accuracy
- spelling and grammar
- client-specific rules
- visual quality
- compliance
- CTA relevance
- repeated mistakes
- missed deliverables

## Capacity

Kydos working rule:

Account Manager:
- 4 clients normally
- up to 5

Creative:
- 4 clients normally
- up to 5

The Operations Manager must intervene before a team member is overloaded enough that quality drops.

## Client escalation

Escalate to owner when:

- client threatens legal action
- client threatens cancellation and commercial intervention is needed
- serious public complaint
- payment dispute beyond normal process
- significant scope dispute
- data/privacy incident
- regulated-content risk
- major ad-spend error
- team misconduct
- material financial risk

## Staff escalation

Use this sequence where appropriate:

1. clarify expectation
2. document problem
3. coach/correct
4. set deadline
5. monitor
6. formal performance action where needed
7. owner/HR/legal escalation where appropriate

## Owner report

A useful weekly owner summary contains:

- active clients
- at-risk clients
- overdue approvals
- overdue delivery
- team capacity
- quality issues
- payment issues
- hiring need
- major wins
- decisions required

The owner should be able to read it in a few minutes.
