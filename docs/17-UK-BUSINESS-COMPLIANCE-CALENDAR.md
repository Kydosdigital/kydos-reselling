# UK Business Compliance Calendar

Last reviewed: 31 August 2026

This is a planning tool. Exact dates depend on the company's incorporation date, accounting period, VAT quarters, payroll and other circumstances.

Use the accountant and official reminders to populate exact dates for each participant.

## At incorporation

- Companies House incorporation
- identity verification
- registered office
- registered email
- shareholders
- PSCs
- SIC code
- company records folder

## As soon as trading starts

- bookkeeping process
- business bank
- accounting software
- accountant
- client contracts
- privacy notice
- ICO fee assessment
- insurance review

## Monthly

- bank reconciliation
- invoice reconciliation
- expense and receipt review
- payroll where applicable
- sales commission review
- tax reserve review
- overdue invoice review
- MRR and margin report

## Every payroll cycle

Where PAYE applies:
- calculate pay
- process deductions
- report to HMRC on or before payday as required
- pay employees
- pay HMRC by the applicable deadline

## VAT

If VAT registered:
- maintain digital records
- prepare VAT return
- submit by applicable deadline
- pay VAT due

Do not assume the VAT quarter matches the calendar quarter.

## Annual Companies House confirmation statement

File at least once every 12 months.

At 31 August 2026:
- online fee: £50
- paper fee: £110

The filing deadline is generally 14 days after the end of the review period.

## Annual accounts

Typical private-company deadlines:
- first accounts: normally 21 months after incorporation
- later annual accounts: normally 9 months after financial year end

## Corporation Tax

Typical timetable:
- pay Corporation Tax: 9 months and 1 day after the Corporation Tax accounting period ends
- file Company Tax Return: 12 months after the accounting period ends

Different or complex circumstances can change the position.

## ICO

Where a data-protection fee is payable:
- diarise annual renewal
- keep company details current

## Insurance

- diarise policy renewal
- review cover before renewal

## Contracts and policies

At least annually:
- review client terms
- review privacy policy
- review employee and contractor terms
- review programme participant terms
- review refund policy
- review software/data processors

## Company changes

Do not wait for the annual confirmation statement to correct everything.

Update Companies House and HMRC when required for changes such as:
- registered office
- directors
- PSCs
- company name
- relevant company records

## Current tax reference points

At 31 August 2026:
- VAT compulsory registration threshold: £90,000 taxable turnover
- Corporation Tax small profits rate: 19% for profits up to £50,000
- Corporation Tax main rate: 25% for profits over £250,000
- marginal relief may apply between £50,000 and £250,000

These are reference points only. Associated companies and other rules can alter the effective thresholds or calculations.
