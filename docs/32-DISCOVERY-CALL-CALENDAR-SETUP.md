# Discovery Call Booking Calendar Setup

The booking calendar must be ready before paid advertising begins.

## Purpose

The calendar gives a lead a quick route to speak with the Sales Closer without needing manual back-and-forth.

## Default calendar

Name:

Discovery Call

Recommended duration:

20 to 30 minutes.

Use a shorter call if the sales process is designed mainly to qualify and recommend a package.

## Availability

Set real working availability.

Do not open appointment times when nobody can answer.

Include:
- working days
- working hours
- minimum notice
- buffer between calls
- maximum calls per day where needed

## Booking form

Collect only information that helps prepare for the call.

Recommended:

- name
- email
- mobile
- business name
- website
- service interest
- short description of what they need

Do not turn the calendar into a 25-question application unless the sales model requires it.

## Confirmation

Immediately after booking:

Email:

"Hi {{first_name}},

Your discovery call with [Agency Name] is confirmed for {{appointment_time}}.

We will use the call to understand your business, what you are trying to achieve and whether one of our services is the right fit.

If you need to reschedule, use the link below.

[Reschedule Link]

Speak soon,

[Agency Name]"

## Reminders

Suggested:

- confirmation immediately
- reminder 24 hours before
- reminder 1 to 2 hours before

Avoid excessive reminders.

## CRM actions

When an appointment is booked:

1. update opportunity stage to Discovery Call Booked
2. assign salesperson
3. notify salesperson
4. add appointment details
5. pause conflicting unbooked-lead follow-up where appropriate

## No-show process

If the prospect does not attend:

1. mark no-show
2. send a short message
3. offer reschedule link
4. make one sensible call attempt
5. move to Follow-Up or Nurture if they do not rebook
