# Sales Closer Interview Guide

## Questions

1. Walk us through how you would respond to a fresh Meta lead.

2. What would you say in the first 20 seconds of the call?

3. How do you qualify without making the call feel like an interrogation?

4. How do you respond to:
"Your competitor is cheaper"?

5. What do you do when someone says:
"I need to think about it"?

6. How do you use a CRM?

7. What does a clean pipeline look like?

8. How do you avoid sounding desperate when following up?

9. When should you disqualify a lead?

10. How do you handle a prospect asking for guaranteed results?

## Roleplay

Run:
- fresh lead call
- price objection
- need-to-think objection
- close and payment handoff

## Red flags

- talks too much
- does not listen
- discounts immediately
- guarantees results
- cannot use a CRM
- poor note taking
- high-pressure tactics
- blames lead quality for everything
