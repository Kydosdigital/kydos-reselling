# Lead Qualification Guide

A lead is not automatically a good client.

## Core qualification areas

### 1. Need

Does the business actually need a service the agency provides?

### 2. Fit

Can the agency realistically help?

### 3. Budget

Can the prospect afford the package and any separate ad spend?

### 4. Authority

Is the person able to make the decision?

### 5. Timing

Do they want to act now?

### 6. Expectations

Are expectations realistic?

Red flag:
"We need you to guarantee 100 customers next month."

### 7. Cooperation

Will the client:
- complete onboarding
- provide assets
- approve content
- respond to questions
- give required platform access

## Questions

- What are you trying to achieve?
- What are you currently doing?
- What has worked before?
- What has not worked?
- What are you spending on marketing now?
- Do you have a separate advertising budget if ads are required?
- Who makes the final decision?
- When do you want to start?
- What would make this successful for you?

## Qualified outcome

Record:
- recommended package
- additional services
- budget
- urgency
- decision-maker
- objections
- next action

## Unqualified outcome

Do not sell simply to collect the first payment if:
- the agency cannot deliver what they need
- expectations are impossible
- the prospect is abusive
- the business is prohibited or creates unacceptable compliance risk
- they cannot afford the service and required media budget
