# Creative Interview Guide

## Portfolio review

Ask the candidate to explain:

- their role in each piece
- brief received
- tools used
- why they made the design choices
- whether the work was approved/published
- what they would improve now

## Questions

1. How do you approach a brand you have never designed for before?

2. How do you decide what text belongs on a graphic and what belongs in the caption?

3. What makes a carousel cover strong?

4. How do you keep video text readable on mobile?

5. What do you do if an Account Manager gives you an unclear brief?

6. How do you organise source files?

7. What is your process before submitting work?

8. What is your strongest tool?

9. What do you use for video editing?

10. Tell us about a correction you received that improved your work.

## Red flags

- heavily template-dependent work with no adaptation
- cannot explain design decisions
- poor typography
- inconsistent spacing
- low-quality exports
- spelling errors
- ignores briefs
- treats feedback as personal criticism
- relies on AI visuals without disclosure
