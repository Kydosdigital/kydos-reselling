# Accountant Referral Process

Kydos can refer programme participants to the accountant used by Kydos where appropriate.

## Purpose

The referral gives the participant a route to a professional who understands the UK business environment and the Kydos operating model.

## Kydos team process

1. Participant asks for accountant support.
2. Confirm consent to share contact details.
3. Send introduction to the accountant.
4. State clearly that the accountant is an independent professional/provider.
5. Participant agrees scope and fees directly with the accountant unless Kydos has explicitly sold a bundled service.
6. Kydos does not promise the accountant's advice, availability or outcome.

## Suggested introduction

Subject: Introduction, [Participant Name] / new UK digital agency

Hi [Accountant],

We would like to introduce [Participant Name], who is setting up a UK digital marketing agency through the Kydos Digital programme.

They would like professional accounting support for their company setup and ongoing compliance.

[Participant], [Accountant] supports UK businesses and can advise you directly on the appropriate accounting, tax, payroll and filing arrangements.

We will leave you both to agree the scope and fees directly.

Kind regards,

Kydos Digital

## Participant should ask about

- monthly accounting fee
- bookkeeping
- annual accounts
- Corporation Tax
- VAT
- PAYE/payroll
- director remuneration
- confirmation statement responsibility
- FreeAgent
- deadline reminders
- sponsor-readiness records where relevant

## Boundary

Kydos provides business education.

The accountant provides professional accounting/tax advice.
