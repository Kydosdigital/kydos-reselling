# Monthly Reporting SOP

Reporting is monthly.

The objective is not to dump platform screenshots on the client.

The report should explain:
- what was delivered
- what happened
- what worked
- what did not
- what should happen next

## Data to collect

Depending on platform/service:

- posts published
- stories published
- follower growth
- reach
- impressions
- engagement
- engagement rate where useful
- profile visits
- link clicks
- video views
- saves
- shares
- comments
- DMs/enquiries
- leads
- top-performing posts

## Report structure

1. Executive summary
2. Deliverables completed
3. Audience growth
4. Reach/visibility
5. Engagement
6. Top-performing content
7. Enquiries/conversions where trackable
8. What we learnt
9. Recommendations
10. Next month's priorities

## Context matters

Do not celebrate a vanity metric without explaining whether it matters.

Example:

Weak:
"Reach increased 40%."

Better:
"Reach increased 40%, driven mainly by two educational reels. However, profile visits remained flat, so next month we should strengthen the CTA and service connection."

## Avoid false attribution

Do not claim:
"Social media generated £20,000"

unless tracking genuinely supports the claim.

## Reporting date

Prepare at month end or agreed monthly reporting date.

## Internal review

Account Manager prepares.

Operations Manager may review higher-risk or strategic accounts.

## Client review

Higher packages may include review meetings according to the package.

All clients receiving reporting should receive the written report.
