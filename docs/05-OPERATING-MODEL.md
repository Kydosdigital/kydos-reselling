# Kydos Agency Operating Model

## Core structure

### Owner

Without an Operations Manager, the owner:
- manages Account Managers and Creatives
- manages specialist contractors
- monitors sales
- monitors finances
- handles major client escalations
- checks delivery quality
- makes hiring decisions
- sets business direction

With an Operations Manager, the owner can focus more on:
- finance
- growth
- hiring decisions
- strategic direction
- leadership

### Operations Manager

The Operations Manager is the day-to-day control layer between the owner and the delivery team.

Core duties include:
- monitoring Account Manager workload
- monitoring Creative workload
- checking deadlines
- reviewing monthly calendars
- overseeing quality control
- escalating serious client issues
- monitoring reports
- ensuring processes are followed
- reporting to the owner

### Account Manager

Owns the client account.

Responsible for:
- client communication
- research
- competitor research
- content strategy
- monthly calendars
- copywriting
- hooks
- captions
- CTAs
- hashtags
- community management where included
- story publishing
- approvals
- follow-up
- monthly reporting
- reactive/trending content decisions
- briefing the Creative

Capacity:
- normal: 4 clients
- maximum working capacity: about 5 clients

### Creative

Owns visual production.

Responsible for:
- static graphics
- carousels
- story designs
- reels
- video edits
- visual execution from the Account Manager's brief

Capacity:
- normal: 4 clients
- maximum working capacity: about 5 clients

### Sales Closer

Responsible for:
- calling leads quickly
- qualifying prospects
- moving leads through the CRM pipeline
- closing the correct package
- maintaining CRM notes
- handing closed clients into onboarding

Commission:
- recurring services: 50% of the first service fee closed, excluding VAT
- one-off work: current working rule approximately 15%, final agreement to confirm

### Specialist Contractors

Used for work that does not need a permanent in-house specialist at launch.

Examples:
- Meta Ads
- Google Ads
- SEO
- website development
- landing pages
- CRM/automation

They are paid based on the work completed or the commercial arrangement agreed with the agency.

## Client workflow

1. Lead enters CRM
2. Lead receives automated follow-up
3. Sales Closer calls within 60 seconds
4. Client agrees package
5. Invoice
6. Payment
7. Agreement
8. Onboarding
9. Account Manager assigned
10. Creative assigned
11. Research
12. Calendar
13. Internal review
14. Client approval
15. Production
16. Quality control
17. Client approval
18. Scheduling/publishing
19. Community management where included
20. Monthly reporting
21. Renewal and retention

## Content workflow

Monthly:
- calendar completed by the 25th
- Operations Manager or CEO reviews
- client receives calendar
- client review target is 3 days
- up to two revision rounds
- client approves

Weekly:
- Account Manager prepares Creative brief
- Creative produces visual assets
- Creative submits by Monday 1:00 PM UK time
- Account Manager reviews first
- material is corrected where required
- client approval is requested
- approved content is scheduled/published

Reactive content:
- Account Manager may replace a planned post with a timely/trending post
- reactive content normally does not increase the monthly content count
