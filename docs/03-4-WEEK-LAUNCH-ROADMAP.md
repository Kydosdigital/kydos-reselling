# 4-Week Agency Launch Roadmap

This is the default route. Customers who need more time can use an 8-week or 12-week version built from the same sequence.

## Week 1, Company foundation

### Day 1
- Choose agency name
- Check domain availability
- Decide initial broad market position
- Review Kydos service model and package structure

### Day 2
- Register the company
- Set up Companies House records
- Store incorporation records securely

### Day 3
- Set up business banking
- Set up FreeAgent
- Engage an accountant

### Day 4
- Buy domain through GoDaddy
- Set up business email
- Create internal Google Drive structure
- Create standard client folder template

### Day 5
- Finalise logo
- Finalise colours and typography
- Create basic brand guide
- Finalise service packages and prices

## Week 2, Systems and recruitment

### Day 6
- Set up branded CRM
- Connect Stripe
- Create sales pipeline
- Create booking calendar

### Day 7
- Build lead follow-up automations
- Email
- SMS
- WhatsApp
- Internal sales call alert

### Day 8
- Create client agreement
- Create invoice process
- Create onboarding email
- Duplicate onboarding Google Form
- Duplicate client Drive structure

### Day 9
- Publish Account Manager vacancy
- Publish Creative vacancy
- Prepare Sales Closer agreement
- Begin candidate screening

### Day 10
- Interview candidates
- Send stage-two test tasks
- Review results
- Prepare offers and contracts

## Week 3, Fulfilment readiness

### Day 11
- Hire Account Manager
- Hire Creative
- Appoint Sales Closer
- Sign agreements
- Begin team onboarding

### Day 12
Train Account Manager on:
- research
- content strategy
- calendars
- copy
- client communication
- community management
- reporting

### Day 13
Train Creative on:
- brand interpretation
- static design
- carousels
- video editing
- weekly creative briefs
- Monday 1:00 PM UK deadline

### Day 14
- Run a dummy client through onboarding
- Build a sample monthly calendar
- Build a sample weekly creative brief
- Run internal quality control

### Day 15
Test:
- CRM lead capture
- email automation
- SMS automation
- WhatsApp automation
- booking
- contract
- invoice
- payment
- onboarding

## Week 4, Client acquisition launch

### Day 16
- Final website check
- Mobile check
- Form test
- Analytics and tracking check
- Privacy and cookie check

### Day 17
- Build Meta Ads campaign
- Create ad copy
- Create creative
- Create lead form or landing page
- Connect CRM

### Day 18
- Test every automation
- Test pipeline stages
- Train sales closer
- Confirm 60-second call process

### Day 19
- Launch Meta Ads
- Monitor enquiries
- Call leads quickly
- Record outcomes

### Day 20
- Review early lead quality
- Improve script
- Improve creative
- Improve targeting
- Confirm delivery team readiness

## Recommended advertising budget

The programme customer should budget at least £300 for their own initial Meta Ads launch.

This ad spend is separate from the programme fee.

## Launch-ready definition

The agency is launch-ready when:

- company is established
- business banking is active or in final setup
- accounting system exists
- domain and email exist
- website exists where included
- CRM works
- Stripe works
- booking works
- contracts work
- onboarding works
- Account Manager is ready
- Creative is ready
- Sales Closer is ready
- Meta Ads campaign is live
- owner understands the daily management routine

## Team model

Minimum launch team:
- Owner
- Account Manager
- Creative
- Sales Closer

More hands-off model:
- Owner
- Operations Manager
- Account Manager
- Creative
- Sales Closer
- specialist contractors as required
