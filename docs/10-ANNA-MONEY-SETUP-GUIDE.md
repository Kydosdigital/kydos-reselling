# ANNA Money Setup Guide

Last reviewed: 31 August 2026

## Why Kydos uses ANNA as the beginner formation route

Kydos originally used ANNA Money because it simplifies the early setup process.

ANNA can:
- submit a limited-company formation
- provide a business account application
- help with company administration through optional services
- offer invoicing and receipt tools
- offer additional tax and company-secretarial products

Kydos later moved its main banking to NatWest, but ANNA remains useful as a beginner-friendly formation route.

## Important commercial note

Kydos will provide participants with its current ANNA referral link or referral code.

The referral details should be inserted into the programme portal, not permanently hard-coded into this document because referral terms can change.

## Setup sequence

### Step 1, Prepare information

Have ready:
- proposed company name
- photo ID
- proof of address
- registered office
- director information
- shareholder information
- PSC information
- SIC code
- business description
- business website or social profile where available

### Step 2, Use Kydos referral

Open the Kydos ANNA referral link or enter the current referral code.

### Step 3, Company formation

Choose the company-registration route and complete the required formation information.

For a standard Kydos-style agency:
- private company limited by shares
- SIC code commonly 73110, Advertising agencies

Review the submission carefully.

### Step 4, Identity verification

Complete all required identity and address checks.

Companies House identity-verification requirements still apply even where an authorised formation provider is used.

### Step 5, Business account

If eligible and desired, complete ANNA's business-account onboarding.

Keep personal and company spending separate.

### Step 6, Save all records

Store:
- confirmation of submission
- certificate of incorporation
- company number
- ANNA account details
- pricing plan selected
- any recurring subscription terms

## When to move or add NatWest

Kydos later moved its main banking to NatWest.

Reasons within the Kydos model:
- conventional UK business banking
- access to FreeAgent with an eligible NatWest business current account
- easier long-term financial administration for Kydos

ANNA does not need to remain the main operating account forever.

## Do not teach this incorrectly

Do not say:
"ANNA is required to start a UK company."

It is not.

Do say:
"Kydos recommends ANNA as a simple formation route because it can combine company registration and business-account onboarding."

Do not say:
"ANNA automatically solves your taxes."

Instead:
"ANNA offers optional tax and admin services, but Kydos still recommends using a suitable accountant where professional tax advice is needed."
