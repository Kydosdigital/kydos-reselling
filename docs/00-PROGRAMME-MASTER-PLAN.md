# Programme Master Plan

## 1. Programme objective

Build a complete UK digital marketing agency business-in-a-box programme based on Kydos Digital Ltd's real operating model.

The programme should take a beginner from zero to having:

1. A registered UK business
2. A business bank account
3. Accounting and bookkeeping software
4. Domain and business email
5. Brand identity
6. Website
7. CRM and sales pipeline
8. Contracts and onboarding systems
9. Service packages and pricing
10. Staff and contractor structure
11. Client acquisition system
12. Content fulfilment system
13. Paid ads fulfilment capability
14. SEO fulfilment capability
15. Website fulfilment capability
16. Reporting and retention process
17. Financial controls
18. Recurring monthly revenue model
19. A route to hiring an Operations Manager and reducing the owner's day-to-day involvement

## 2. Target customer

Primary audience:
- People in the UK who want to start a UK digital marketing agency
- Complete beginners
- People who want a recurring-revenue service business
- People who want a business that can become owner-led and more hands-off over time

Secondary audience:
- People outside the UK who want to establish a UK agency, subject to legal and banking eligibility
- People exploring whether a genuine UK operating company may form part of a future immigration strategy

The immigration angle is not the core sales promise.

## 6. Kydos sales journey

1. Prospect sees Meta Ad
2. Prospect submits enquiry
3. Lead enters CRM
4. Automated email sent
5. Automated SMS sent
6. Automated WhatsApp sent
7. Sales agent calls within 60 seconds
8. Sales agent moves lead through pipeline
9. Prospect agrees to package
10. Invoice generated
11. Prospect pays
12. Agreement signed
13. Onboarding begins

Sales closer commission:
- 50% of the first service fee closed
- VAT excluded from commission basis

## 9. Social media fulfilment model

### Account Manager

Owns:
- Client relationship
- Business research
- Competitor research
- Content strategy
- Monthly calendar
- Copywriting
- Captions
- Hooks
- CTAs
- Hashtags
- Community management according to package
- Story publishing
- Client communication
- Approval chasing
- Reporting
- Reactive content decisions

Capacity:
- 4 clients normally
- Up to 5 clients

### Creative

Owns:
- Static design
- Carousel design
- Story design
- Video editing
- Reel editing
- Creative execution from Account Manager brief

Capacity:
- 4 clients normally
- Up to 5 clients

Current creative tools include:
- CapCut
- Freepik
- Envato
- Photoshop
- Adobe tools
- Canva where the output remains professional

### Content deadlines

- Following month's content calendar due internally by the 25th
- Operations Manager or CEO reviews before client
- Client approval target is 3 days
- Two revision rounds
- Account Manager prepares weekly creative brief after calendar approval
- Creative weekly batch due Monday by 1:00 PM UK time
- Reactive or trending posts normally replace a scheduled post rather than increasing the monthly content count
- Reporting is monthly

## 10. Staff recruitment model

Before advertising for clients, the new agency should have its basic delivery capacity in place.

Initial structure:
- Owner
- Account Manager
- Creative
- Sales closer
- Specialist contractors for Meta Ads, Google Ads, SEO and website work as required
- Operations Manager can be added to create a more hands-off model

Recruitment channels currently used by Kydos:
- Account Managers: LinkedIn
- Creatives: recruiter/contact sourced through X/Twitter

Account Manager hiring priorities:
- Strong copywriting
- Strong research
- Can produce a professional social media content calendar
- Understands hooks
- Understands client business and audience
- Can communicate professionally with clients

Hiring process includes an unpaid stage-two test task.

## 13. Sponsor licence and immigration information

This is a secondary informational pathway only.

The programme may explain:
- Why a genuine operating UK company matters
- Typical company records and systems that demonstrate real trading
- Payroll and PAYE where relevant
- Business banking and accounting
- Sponsor licence readiness as a business process
- The need for current Home Office requirements to be checked
- The need for regulated immigration advice for personal visa strategy

The programme must not market "self-sponsorship" as guaranteed or treat it as a visa category.
