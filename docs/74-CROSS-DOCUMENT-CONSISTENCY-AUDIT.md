## FLAG, client continuation and cancellation mechanics

Commercial intention recorded:

- first paid month
- then 3, 6 or 12-month continuation options
- one-month notice is also referenced in Kydos operating rules

The final client contract must clarify exactly how one-month notice interacts with a 3, 6 or 12-month fixed commitment.

This is a legal/commercial drafting issue and should not be improvised by Sales staff.

## PASS, payment grace period

Current Kydos operating approach:

approximately 14 days before service-pause consequences for an unresolved missed payment.

Final B2B terms preserve applicable statutory late-payment rights.

## PASS, programme legal boundary

Programme documents consistently state that:

- business results are not guaranteed
- tax advice goes to an accountant
- legal documents require solicitor review
- immigration questions go to a regulated immigration adviser/solicitor
- there is no formal immigration route called self-sponsorship
