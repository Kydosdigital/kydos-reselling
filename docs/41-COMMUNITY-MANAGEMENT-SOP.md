# Community Management SOP

Community management is delivered according to the client's package.

## What it includes

Depending on package:
- reply to comments
- reply to simple DMs
- acknowledge mentions
- engage with relevant audience/community
- escalate sales enquiries
- escalate complaints
- hide/report obvious spam where appropriate

## Tone

Match the client's voice.

Be:
- helpful
- human
- concise
- professional

## Sales enquiries

If someone asks:
"How much?"
"How do I book?"
"Can someone call me?"

Respond with the approved next step and record/pass the lead where necessary.

## Complaints

Do not argue.

Suggested first response:

"Thanks for raising this. We're sorry to hear about your experience. Please send us a direct message with your details so the team can look into it properly."

Then escalate.

## Sensitive issues

Escalate:
- legal threats
- safeguarding
- medical issues
- discrimination allegations
- serious service complaints
- refund disputes
- media enquiries
- threatening messages
- data/privacy complaints

## Spam

May:
- hide obvious spam
- block repeated malicious spam
- report scams

Do not delete legitimate criticism simply because it is negative.

## Engagement

Where included:
- interact with genuinely relevant accounts
- respond to community discussion
- avoid fake engagement
- avoid meaningless emoji spam

## Response time

Use the service/package expectation.

Do not promise 24/7 response unless the agency actually staffs it.

## Record important interactions

Capture:
- serious complaint
- sales lead
- partnership enquiry
- testimonial
- influencer enquiry
- press enquiry
