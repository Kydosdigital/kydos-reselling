# Finance Monthly Routine

This routine is deliberately simple enough for an agency owner to follow even when an accountant handles the formal filings.

## Weekly

- [ ] Check bank balance
- [ ] Check Stripe settlements
- [ ] Check overdue client invoices
- [ ] Upload missing receipts
- [ ] Review unusual expenses
- [ ] Confirm payroll or contractor payment commitments
- [ ] Confirm upcoming software renewals

## Month end

### Revenue

- [ ] Record total revenue
- [ ] Record recurring service revenue
- [ ] Record one-off revenue
- [ ] Record VAT separately where relevant
- [ ] Compare CRM invoices with accounting records
- [ ] Confirm all Stripe payments reconciled

### Clients

- [ ] Count active recurring clients
- [ ] Count new clients
- [ ] Count cancelled clients
- [ ] Calculate MRR
- [ ] Calculate churn
- [ ] List overdue clients
- [ ] List clients in payment grace period

### Delivery cost

- [ ] Account Manager cost
- [ ] Creative cost
- [ ] Operations Manager cost
- [ ] sales commissions
- [ ] specialist contractor cost
- [ ] software cost
- [ ] advertising cost
- [ ] professional fees

### Margin

Calculate:

Gross profit = Revenue minus direct fulfilment cost

Gross margin % = Gross profit / Revenue x 100

Do not mistake money in the bank for profit.

### Tax

- [ ] Review expected Corporation Tax position with accountant or accounting software
- [ ] Review VAT due where registered
- [ ] Review PAYE/NIC due where relevant
- [ ] Move money into tax reserve if the agency uses a separate reserve account

### Cash

- [ ] Confirm enough cash for next payroll/contractor cycle
- [ ] Confirm enough cash for software
- [ ] Confirm enough cash for ad spend
- [ ] Confirm expected tax obligations
- [ ] Identify any large one-off payment due

## Monthly management summary

Record these numbers:

- Revenue
- MRR
- One-off revenue
- New clients
- Lost clients
- Client churn %
- Direct fulfilment cost
- Gross profit
- Gross margin %
- Sales commissions
- Advertising spend
- Cash balance
- Overdue invoices
- Tax reserve
- Number of clients per Account Manager
- Number of clients per Creative

## Quarterly

- [ ] Review package profitability
- [ ] Review prices
- [ ] Review staffing capacity
- [ ] Cancel unused software
- [ ] Review insurance
- [ ] Review client acquisition cost
- [ ] Review cash reserve target
- [ ] Review whether another hire is affordable

## Annual

Coordinate with the accountant and compliance calendar for:
- annual accounts
- Corporation Tax return
- confirmation statement
- payroll year-end
- insurance renewal
- ICO fee renewal where applicable
- contracts and policy review
