# Meta Ads Service SOP

This SOP teaches the agency owner how to sell, scope, hand off, quality-check and report Meta Ads management.

It is not a full media-buying course.

If the owner wants to personally become a Meta Ads specialist, that is a separate training product.

## Kydos pricing

- £150/month management where monthly ad spend is up to £500
- 15% of monthly ad spend where spend exceeds £500

Ad spend is paid by the client and is separate from the management fee.

## Delivery model

At launch, Meta Ads can be fulfilled by a specialist contractor.

The agency still owns:
- client relationship
- scope
- onboarding
- approvals
- billing
- reporting quality
- contractor management
- commercial accountability

## Before accepting a client

Confirm:

- business is suitable for Meta advertising
- offer is clear
- destination or lead form is ready
- client has a realistic ad budget
- client understands ad spend is separate
- tracking/access can be provided
- sales follow-up exists
- prohibited or restricted category issues are understood

## Onboarding

Collect:

- business details
- objective
- target geography
- audience
- offer
- priority service/product
- monthly ad budget
- existing account history
- Meta Business access
- ad account access
- Page access
- Instagram access
- Pixel/dataset access
- website/landing page
- CRM details
- creative assets
- compliance restrictions
- lead follow-up process

Use the Meta Ads Onboarding Form.

## Campaign planning

The specialist should document:

- business objective
- campaign objective
- conversion event or lead mechanism
- audience
- geography
- placements
- budget
- creative angles
- offer
- landing destination
- tracking
- CRM handoff

## Creative

Every ad creative should be approved.

Test useful variation:
- hook
- image/video
- headline
- primary text
- CTA
- offer angle

Do not change five things at once and then pretend to know what caused the result.

## Launch QA

Before spending:

- correct ad account
- correct currency/timezone
- correct Page/Instagram
- correct campaign objective
- correct budget
- correct geography
- correct landing page or lead form
- correct pixel/dataset/event
- CRM lead delivery tested
- notifications tested
- copy checked
- creative checked
- privacy wording checked
- tracking parameters checked where used

## Optimisation

The specialist should review:
- spend
- delivery
- CPM
- CTR
- CPC
- cost per lead/conversion
- lead quality
- frequency
- creative performance
- audience performance
- landing-page performance where relevant
- sales feedback

Do not optimise only around the cheapest lead.

A cheap lead that never answers or cannot buy may be worse than a more expensive qualified lead.

## Sales feedback loop

Account Manager/Sales Closer should report:

- contact rate
- qualified rate
- booked calls
- show rate
- closed clients
- reasons leads are poor

This data should inform campaign decisions.

## Reporting

Monthly.

Include:
- spend
- results
- cost per result
- lead quality
- best creative
- audience insight
- landing/CRM issues
- sales outcomes where known
- next actions

## Escalation

Escalate when:
- account restricted
- spend error
- tracking breaks
- leads stop flowing
- client asks for prohibited claims
- budget changes materially
- lead quality collapses
- sales team is not following up

## Agency owner rule

Do not sell Meta Ads management if nobody competent is responsible for the actual media buying.
