# Revision SOP

Standard Kydos content allowance:

2 revision rounds.

## What is a revision round?

A round is one consolidated set of changes to the submitted work.

Example:

Round 1:
Client sends five requested edits together.

The team completes them and resubmits.

Round 2:
Client sends final consolidated corrections.

## Not a new round

A correction caused by the agency's own clear error should not be counted unfairly against the client.

Examples:
- spelling mistake
- wrong phone number
- wrong logo
- failure to follow approved brief

## Out-of-scope changes

Examples:
- completely new concept after approval
- new campaign direction
- new pages/posts beyond package
- reshooting video
- major animation not in brief
- repeated changes after two rounds

Account Manager should:
1. explain the scope
2. offer a quotation where appropriate
3. obtain approval before extra work

## Feedback quality

Ask clients to be specific.

Weak:
"I don't like it."

Better:
"The cover feels too corporate. Please use the brighter visual style from the approved examples and reduce the text."

## Record permanent corrections

If feedback could apply again:
update the Client Content Rules Sheet.
