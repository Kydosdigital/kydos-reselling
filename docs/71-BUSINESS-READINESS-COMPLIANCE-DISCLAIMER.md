# Business Readiness Compliance Disclaimer

## Purpose

The Kydos Digital Agency Business-in-a-Box programme provides practical education and implementation support for starting and operating a UK digital marketing agency.

## Not legal advice

Templates, checklists, explanations and examples are not a substitute for advice from a solicitor.

Legal documents should be reviewed for the participant's actual business and circumstances where necessary.

## Not tax or accounting advice

Kydos can explain:
- how Kydos manages finance operationally
- which records an agency should maintain
- common business processes
- how to use business software

The participant should obtain tax/accounting advice from a qualified accountant for:
- VAT
- Corporation Tax
- PAYE
- payroll
- director remuneration
- personal tax
- accounting treatment

## Not immigration advice

The programme may include general information about:
- operating a genuine UK business
- sponsor-licence business readiness
- HR systems
- record keeping
- payroll readiness
- current public Home Office guidance

It does not determine an individual's immigration eligibility.

There is no guarantee of:
- sponsor licence
- Certificate of Sponsorship
- Skilled Worker permission
- visa switching
- settlement

Participants with immigration questions should use the Kydos referral to a suitably regulated immigration adviser or solicitor.

## Rules change

UK company, tax, employment, data protection and immigration rules can change.

Documents that contain current thresholds or fees should include a review date.

Before acting on a time-sensitive requirement, check the current official source.

## Business results

Kydos does not guarantee:
- number of clients
- turnover
- profit
- MRR
- ad performance
- time to first sale

The programme gives the participant systems, processes, support and implementation according to the package purchased.

Commercial results depend on execution, market, pricing, offer, sales ability, budget, staff performance and other factors.
