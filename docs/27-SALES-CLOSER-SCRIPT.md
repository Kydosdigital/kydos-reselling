# Sales Closer Script

This script is a framework, not something to read robotically.

## 1. Opening

"Hi [Name], it's [Name] from [Agency]. You recently enquired about help with your marketing. Have you got a couple of minutes?"

## 2. Permission and context

"What made you reach out today?"

Let them speak.

## 3. Understand the business

Ask naturally:

- What does the business do?
- Who are your customers?
- Which service/product are you trying to grow?
- What are you doing for marketing at the moment?
- What is not working?
- Have you worked with an agency or freelancer before?
- What would a good result look like?

## 4. Establish urgency

"Is this something you want to start now, or are you still researching options?"

## 5. Recommend, do not dump every package

Example:

"Based on what you've told me, I wouldn't put you on the biggest package just for the sake of it. I think [package] makes the most sense because [reason]."

## 6. Explain the offer

Cover:
- deliverables
- platforms
- frequency
- support
- reporting
- what is not included
- ad spend separately where relevant

## 7. Price

"The monthly fee for that is £[amount] plus VAT where applicable."

Then stop talking and let the prospect respond.

## 8. Objections

Use the Objection Handling Guide.

Do not:
- argue
- promise guaranteed sales
- invent scarcity
- discount without authority
- misrepresent deliverables

## 9. Close

"If you're happy to go ahead, I can send the payment link and agreement now. Once that's completed, we can start onboarding you straight away."

## 10. After close

Immediately:
- update CRM
- send invoice/payment link
- send agreement
- confirm payment
- trigger onboarding

## Sales principle

The job is not to force a sale.

The job is to:
- understand
- qualify
- recommend
- explain
- close the right customer
