# ICO and Data Protection Setup Guide

Last reviewed: 31 August 2026

Digital agencies handle personal data.

Examples:
- client names
- client emails
- phone numbers
- Meta lead-form submissions
- CRM contacts
- website enquiries
- staff records
- recruitment applications
- analytics identifiers

Data protection must therefore be part of launch, not an afterthought.

## 1. Assess whether the ICO data-protection fee applies

Use the ICO's official self-assessment.

Not every organisation must pay a fee, but many businesses acting as data controllers do.

Current ICO fee tiers at 31 August 2026 include:
- Tier 1: £52
- Tier 2: £78
- Tier 3: £3,763

A £5 discount is currently available for Direct Debit.

The correct tier depends on factors including turnover, staff numbers and organisation type.

Do not guess. Use the ICO assessment.

## 2. Kydos position

Kydos is registered with the ICO and pays the data-protection fee.

Programme customers should assess and complete their own registration rather than simply copying the Kydos fee tier.

## 3. Documents the agency should have

At minimum:
- privacy notice
- website privacy policy
- cookie information and consent where required
- client data-processing terms where relevant
- staff confidentiality/data policy
- contractor confidentiality/data policy
- retention policy
- breach-response process
- subject-rights process
- access-control policy

## 4. Data map

Create a simple record of:

What data do we collect?
Where does it come from?
Why do we need it?
Where is it stored?
Who can access it?
Who is it shared with?
How long do we keep it?
How is it deleted?

Systems may include:
- CRM
- Google Drive
- Google Forms
- Meta
- Google
- Stripe
- email
- website
- analytics
- social platforms

## 5. Client credentials

Kydos historically stored client access information inside restricted Google Drive client folders.

For the programme, the recommended future standard should be stronger:
- use delegated platform access where available
- use a reputable password manager for credentials
- do not store plain-text passwords in open documents
- do not send passwords in ordinary WhatsApp conversations
- remove access promptly when staff leave

A password-manager selection will be added to the technology stack before final publication.

## 6. Staff access

Use least privilege.

An Account Manager should only access clients they manage.

A Creative should only access the files and assets needed for production.

Operations Manager access can be broader because of quality control.

The owner controls administrative access.

## 7. Lead generation

When collecting leads:
- clearly identify who is collecting the data
- provide appropriate privacy information
- do not add people to unrelated marketing without a lawful basis
- respect opt-outs
- configure CRM permissions carefully

## 8. Data breach

If data is lost, disclosed to the wrong person or accessed improperly:
1. contain the incident
2. preserve evidence
3. assess risk
4. document the incident
5. decide whether ICO notification is required
6. decide whether affected people must be notified
7. fix the cause

Seek professional advice for serious incidents.
