# Invoice and Payment Workflow

## Kydos commercial principle

Work starts after payment.

Monthly recurring services are paid in advance.

## Recurring service

1. Salesperson confirms package.
2. CRM product/price selected.
3. VAT applied correctly where applicable.
4. Payment link/invoice sent.
5. Customer pays.
6. CRM marks payment.
7. Accounting record is reconciled.
8. Agreement completed.
9. Onboarding starts.
10. Future recurring billing is scheduled.

## One-off service

Examples:
- website
- landing page

Current programme commercial rule:
Full payment before work begins unless a different written project agreement is approved.

## Invoice fields

Include:
- agency legal/trading name
- invoice number
- invoice date
- customer details
- service
- service period
- net amount
- VAT where applicable
- gross amount
- payment terms
- payment method

## Failed recurring payment

Suggested Kydos process:

Day 0:
- automated failure notice

Early follow-up:
- Account Manager/finance follows up

Grace period:
- approximately two weeks under the current Kydos model

If unresolved:
- warn client of service pause
- pause according to agreement
- record all communication

## No quiet write-offs

Every overdue amount must end in one of these states:
- paid
- payment plan formally agreed
- refunded/credited
- written off with owner approval
- service cancelled
- debt recovery/escalation

## Sales commission

Recurring services:
50% of the first service fee closed, excluding VAT.

One-off services:
use the approved one-off commission rule in the Sales Closer Agreement.

Commission should only be calculated from money actually received, not verbal promises.
