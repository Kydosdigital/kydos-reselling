# Account Manager Interview Guide

## Screening questions

1. Tell us about the client accounts you have managed.

2. How do you research a business before writing content?

3. What makes a content calendar strategic rather than just a list of posts?

4. Give an example of a strong hook for a boring/technical business.

5. How do you adapt tone between a therapist, restaurant and B2B software company?

6. What do you do when a client says:
"I don't like this"
without explaining why?

7. How do you prevent the same client correction from happening again?

8. What would you include in a weekly Creative brief?

9. How do you decide whether a trend is worth using for a client?

10. How do you report social performance without exaggerating?

## Practical judgment scenarios

### Scenario 1

The Creative's weekly batch is due today. The client has still not approved the calendar.

What do you do?

Strong answer:
Do not tell the Creative to produce unapproved concepts. Chase approval, document the delay, communicate timeline impact, escalate if needed.

### Scenario 2

A client says:
"Make the caption more viral."

What questions do you ask?

### Scenario 3

A health client wants a strong medical claim that you cannot verify.

What do you do?

Strong answer:
Do not publish it as fact. Check source/compliance and escalate.

## Red flags

- cannot explain research
- writes generic copy
- thinks a calendar is only dates and captions
- poor written communication
- dismisses client feedback
- relies completely on AI
- no quality-control habits
- weak deadline ownership
