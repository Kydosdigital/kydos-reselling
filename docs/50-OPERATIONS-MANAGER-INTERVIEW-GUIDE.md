# Operations Manager Interview Guide

## Core questions

1. How would you manage four Account Managers and four Creatives across multiple client deadlines?

2. What information would you want on an operations dashboard?

3. How do you distinguish a one-off mistake from a performance problem?

4. A client is angry because content is late. What do you do in the first hour?

5. An Account Manager produces weak calendars every month despite feedback. What is your process?

6. Two Account Managers need the same Creative urgently. How do you prioritise?

7. How do you prevent the owner becoming the default person for every problem?

8. What should be escalated to the owner?

9. How do you run probation reviews?

10. How would you report agency operations weekly?

## Scenario

It is Monday 12:30 PM.

Three Creatives are due to submit at 1:00 PM.

One has warned that two client batches will be late.

One Account Manager has not responded to messages.

A client has also asked to cancel.

Ask the candidate to explain:
- priority order
- communication
- escalation
- documentation
- prevention

## Strong candidate traits

- prioritises by business/client risk
- communicates early
- uses process, not panic
- can challenge staff
- protects quality
- understands capacity
- keeps owner informed without forwarding every small issue
