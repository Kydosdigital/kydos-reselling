# FreeAgent Setup Guide

Last reviewed: 31 August 2026

## Kydos accounting stack

Kydos uses:
- NatWest
- FreeAgent
- an external accountant
- CRM-generated client invoices and Stripe payments

FreeAgent is the accounting record and bookkeeping environment, not a replacement for professional advice.

## Initial setup

### Company details

Enter:
- legal company name
- company number
- registered office
- accounting year end
- VAT status
- Corporation Tax details
- PAYE details where relevant

Have the accountant verify tax settings.

### Connect bank

Connect the NatWest business account using the supported bank feed.

Review imported transactions regularly.

### Add accountant

Invite the accountant through the correct access method.

Do not send your personal login password.

### Opening balances

If the business has already traded before FreeAgent setup, have the accountant confirm opening balances and any historic transactions that must be entered.

## Chart of accounts and categories

Keep categories understandable.

Typical agency costs include:
- advertising
- contractor costs
- wages and salaries
- software subscriptions
- web hosting and domains
- professional fees
- insurance
- bank fees
- telephone and internet
- travel where allowable
- office costs
- training
- equipment

Ask the accountant before creating unusual tax categories.

## Sales

Kydos invoices through its CRM, but accounting records still need to match money received.

For each payment:
- preserve invoice reference
- record gross amount
- record VAT correctly where applicable
- record Stripe fees
- reconcile net settlement
- keep refund evidence where relevant

## Expenses

For every business expense:
- use company funds where practical
- keep the receipt or invoice
- upload evidence
- use a clear category
- explain unusual costs

## VAT

Kydos is VAT registered.

A new agency does not automatically need VAT registration simply because it is a limited company.

Current compulsory registration threshold at 31 August 2026:
£90,000 taxable turnover, subject to the detailed HMRC rules.

Voluntary registration may be possible below the threshold.

The accountant should advise whether and when to register.

## Corporation Tax

At 31 August 2026, headline Corporation Tax rules include:
- 19% small profits rate for companies with profits of £50,000 or less
- 25% main rate for profits above £250,000
- marginal relief may apply between those levels

These thresholds can be affected by factors such as associated companies and accounting-period length.

Do not use a simple calculator as the final Corporation Tax return.

## Payroll and PAYE

If the company employs people through UK payroll:
- register as an employer when required
- choose payroll software or use accountant payroll
- report pay and deductions
- make payments to HMRC on time
- maintain employee records

Kydos uses PAYE where UK payroll is relevant.

Overseas contractor arrangements are separate and should be documented correctly for the worker's jurisdiction.
