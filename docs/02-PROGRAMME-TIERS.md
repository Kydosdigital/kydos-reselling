## Payment

All programme tiers are paid in full before work or access begins.

## Refund policy direction

A full participant agreement, terms and refund policy will be drafted.

The policy must protect Kydos while remaining consistent with applicable UK consumer law. Digital access, bespoke work already started, services already delivered and statutory rights must be handled clearly rather than relying on a blanket statement that no refund is ever possible.

## Programme support model

- Blueprint: standard WhatsApp support, 8 weeks
- Build With Us: priority implementation support, 12 weeks
- Done For You: priority setup and handover support, including 90 days after handover
