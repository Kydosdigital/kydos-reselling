# Recruitment Master SOP

This SOP covers the standard Kydos-style hiring process for Account Managers, Creatives, Operations Managers and Sales Closers.

The programme teaches agency owners to recruit before paid client acquisition begins so the business has delivery capacity when leads convert.

## Core hiring principle

Do not hire only because someone is available.

Hire because:
- the role is clearly defined
- the person can demonstrate the required skill
- the person understands the working standard
- the economics of the role make sense
- the agency can manage them properly

## Minimum launch team

Recommended launch structure:

- Owner
- Account Manager
- Creative
- Sales Closer

More hands-off structure:

- Owner
- Operations Manager
- Account Manager
- Creative
- Sales Closer
- specialist contractors as required

## Recruitment channels

Kydos currently uses:

Account Managers:
- LinkedIn

Creatives:
- Kydos recruitment contact/network on X/Twitter

Sales Closers:
- direct sourcing, referrals or specialist recruitment

Operations Managers:
- LinkedIn, referrals or specialist recruitment

## Hiring stages

### Stage 1, Application review

Check:
- relevant experience
- writing ability
- communication quality
- examples of work
- attention to detail
- professionalism
- location/time-zone suitability
- realistic availability

### Stage 2, Screening interview

Assess:
- understanding of role
- communication
- previous experience
- problem solving
- reliability
- ownership
- ability to follow process

### Stage 3, Test task

Kydos uses an unpaid stage-two test task for selected roles.

The task must be:
- clearly described
- time-bounded
- proportionate
- not disguised free client work

### Stage 4, Final interview

Review:
- test task
- judgment
- feedback response
- working expectations
- compensation
- probation
- confidentiality
- availability

### Stage 5, Offer

Send:
- offer letter
- role agreement/contract
- confidentiality/IP terms
- data protection obligations
- start date
- working schedule
- probation terms

### Stage 6, Onboarding

Provide:
- role manual
- SOPs
- access
- communication channels
- client assignment rules
- deadlines
- reporting structure
- test account or shadow work where appropriate

## Probation

Kydos working standard:
- 3 months probation

The final role agreement should state:
- notice during probation
- notice after probation
- performance expectations
- review points

## Quality bar

A candidate should not be hired because:
"we can train everything later."

Training supports a competent person.

It should not compensate for the absence of the core skill the role exists to provide.

## Recruitment records

Store:
- CV
- application
- interview notes
- test task
- scorecard
- offer
- signed agreement
- ID/verification records where lawfully required
- references where used

Keep candidate data only as long as needed and in line with the recruitment privacy notice.
