## Trigger-based selling

Cross-sell when a real problem appears.

Examples:

Problem:
Leads arrive but are not followed up.

Offer:
CRM + automation.

Problem:
Ads get clicks but page converts poorly.

Offer:
Landing-page rebuild.

Problem:
Business relies entirely on paid traffic.

Offer:
SEO.

## Account Manager role

Account Manager identifies opportunity.

Sales/owner may handle commercial close depending on structure.

Record the recommendation in CRM.
