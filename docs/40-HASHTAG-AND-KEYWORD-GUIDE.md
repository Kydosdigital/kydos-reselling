# Hashtag and Keyword Guide

Hashtags are not the strategy.

They are one small part of content discoverability.

## Use a mix

Possible groups:

- brand
- service
- industry
- location
- audience
- topic

Example:

Brand:
#YourAgency

Service:
#SocialMediaManagement

Industry:
#HospitalityMarketing

Location:
#ManchesterBusiness

Audience:
#SmallBusinessUK

Topic:
#ContentStrategy

## Do not

- paste the same large block on every post
- use irrelevant trending hashtags
- use tags only because they have millions of posts
- rely on hashtags instead of clear captions and keywords

## Platform keywords

Write naturally for what people may search.

Example:

Instead of:
"We help you get seen."

Prefer:
"Social media management for Manchester restaurants."

Where appropriate, use the service/location naturally in:
- caption
- profile
- title
- on-screen text
- alt text
- video speech/subtitles

## Hashtag quantity

Use a sensible, relevant number based on the platform and current practice.

Do not build the programme around a fixed number that may become outdated.

## Research

Before using a hashtag:
- check relevance
- check whether the content under it matches the audience
- avoid banned/spam-heavy terms

## Branded hashtags

Use when there is a reason:
- campaign
- event
- community
- series

Do not invent a branded hashtag simply to tick a box.
