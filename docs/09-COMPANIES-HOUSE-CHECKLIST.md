# Companies House Checklist

Last reviewed: 31 August 2026

Use this checklist when creating a new agency company.

## Before registration

- [ ] Confirm private limited company is the chosen structure
- [ ] Choose company name
- [ ] Search Companies House for same or similar names
- [ ] Check UK trade marks
- [ ] Check domain availability
- [ ] Select an appropriate UK registered office
- [ ] Create registered email
- [ ] Confirm director or directors
- [ ] Confirm shareholder or shareholders
- [ ] Identify PSCs
- [ ] Decide share structure
- [ ] Select SIC code, Kydos model uses 73110 Advertising agencies
- [ ] Prepare identity documents
- [ ] Complete Companies House identity verification where required

## Registration

Recommended Kydos beginner route:
- [ ] Use current Kydos ANNA referral link or code
- [ ] Complete ANNA formation questions
- [ ] Review all details before submission
- [ ] Submit incorporation

Alternative:
- [ ] Incorporate directly through Companies House

Current Companies House digital incorporation fee at 31 August 2026: £100.

## Immediately after incorporation

- [ ] Download certificate of incorporation
- [ ] Record company number
- [ ] Record incorporation date
- [ ] Store articles of association
- [ ] Store memorandum
- [ ] Store statement of capital
- [ ] Store shareholder details
- [ ] Store PSC details
- [ ] Store Companies House personal codes securely
- [ ] Obtain and securely store authentication code
- [ ] Create Company Admin / Companies House folder

## Ongoing Companies House responsibilities

- [ ] Keep registered office current
- [ ] Keep registered email current
- [ ] Update director changes
- [ ] Update PSC changes
- [ ] Update share information where required
- [ ] File annual confirmation statement
- [ ] File annual accounts
- [ ] Monitor Companies House email and post

## Confirmation statement

A confirmation statement confirms that company information is accurate.

As of 31 August 2026:
- online annual fee: £50
- paper fee: £110

The statement can be filed within 14 days after the end of the review period.

Do not confuse the confirmation statement with annual accounts.

## Accounts

For a private limited company, common deadlines include:
- first accounts: normally 21 months after incorporation
- later annual accounts: normally 9 months after the financial year ends

Corporation Tax and Company Tax Return have different deadlines.

Use the compliance calendar and accountant to track all dates.

## Identity verification

From 18 November 2025, identity verification became a legal requirement for relevant directors and PSCs, with transition arrangements for existing appointments.

The programme should always use the current Companies House process, because the rollout may continue to evolve.
