# Sponsor Licence Business Readiness Guide

Last reviewed: 31 August 2026

This section is optional and informational.

It does not promise that the business owner will qualify for a sponsor licence, a Skilled Worker visa or any other immigration route.

There is no visa category formally called "self-sponsorship".

The business must be genuine, operating and able to comply with Home Office sponsor duties. Any personal immigration strategy must be reviewed by a suitably regulated immigration adviser or solicitor.

## What this guide is for

This guide explains how a genuine digital marketing agency should keep itself operationally ready if it later considers applying for a sponsor licence.

The goal is not to manufacture paperwork for an immigration application.

The goal is to run a real business properly from day one.

## 1. Build a real operating company

The agency should be able to demonstrate genuine business activity through normal records such as:

- Companies House records
- UK business bank account
- invoices
- client contracts
- supplier/contractor agreements
- accounting records
- website
- business email
- CRM
- marketing activity
- client delivery
- payroll where relevant
- tax registrations where relevant
- insurance
- staff structure
- business premises/registered office records where relevant

Do not create fake contracts, fake invoices, fabricated turnover or sham roles.

## 2. Business bank account

For a business that has been operating or trading in the UK for less than 18 months, current Home Office Appendix A guidance requires evidence of a corporate or business account with a UK bank or building society that is authorised and regulated by the Prudential Regulation Authority and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority.

The most recent fully itemised statement is normally required for this startup evidence.

Kydos uses NatWest as its main business bank, but NatWest itself is not the immigration requirement.

## 3. Supporting documents

Most organisations applying for a Worker or Temporary Worker sponsor licence currently need a minimum of 4 documents or combinations of documents, subject to route-specific and organisation-specific rules.

For Skilled Worker applications, most ordinary businesses must also provide additional information about the organisation and the jobs they intend to fill.

This can include:

- why the business is applying
- business sector
- normal operating hours
- hierarchy chart
- employee list where the organisation has 50 employees or fewer
- current vacancies/jobs intended to be filled
- details of proposed sponsored workers where known

Always check the current Appendix A immediately before application.

## 4. Key personnel

A sponsor-licence application requires key personnel.

Current roles include:

- Authorising Officer
- Key Contact
- Level 1 User

A Level 2 User may be added later where appropriate.

The same person may hold more than one role if eligible.

Key personnel must meet Home Office suitability and eligibility rules.

Do not appoint someone to a sponsor role simply because they know the login.

## 5. HR systems

The business must have systems capable of supporting sponsor duties.

This includes processes to:

- monitor immigration status
- retain required right-to-work evidence
- keep contact details current
- monitor attendance where relevant
- keep employment records
- report relevant changes
- track salary/job changes
- maintain sponsored-worker files
- manage Certificates of Sponsorship correctly

The Operations Manager or another competent person may help maintain these systems, but legal responsibility remains with the sponsor.

## 6. Genuine role

A Skilled Worker role must be genuine.

Current guidance makes clear that a role should not:

- be fictitious
- be a sham
- be created mainly so someone can obtain immigration permission

The role must also satisfy the occupation-code, skill and salary rules applying at the time.

## 7. Salary

Do not hard-code one salary into the programme as if it applies to every role.

As at 31 August 2026, the normal Skilled Worker salary threshold is generally the higher of:

- £41,700 per year
- the applicable going rate for the occupation

There are exceptions and alternative salary rules in some circumstances.

The immigration adviser must check the proposed role, occupation code and current salary rules.

## 8. Sponsor-licence fee

As at 31 August 2026, the GOV.UK employer guidance lists:

- Worker sponsor licence, small/charitable sponsor: £611
- Worker sponsor licence, medium/large sponsor: £1,682

Fees can change.

The sponsor must pay its own licence fee. Do not pass prohibited sponsor-licence application costs to a sponsored worker.

## 9. Compliance duties continue after approval

A sponsor licence is not a one-time certificate.

The business must continue complying with sponsor duties.

UKVI may conduct compliance checks and can downgrade, suspend or revoke a licence where duties are not met.

## 10. Kydos programme route

Where a participant expresses immigration interest:

1. Build the agency as a genuine commercial business.
2. Keep proper records.
3. Use the accountant for tax/payroll readiness.
4. Use Kydos's immigration solicitor/adviser referral for immigration-specific advice.
5. Do not make an immigration application based solely on this programme document.
6. Verify the latest Home Office requirements before taking action.
