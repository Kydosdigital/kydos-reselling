# Story SOP

Story delivery depends on the client's package.

## Responsibilities

Account Manager:
- decides what should be posted
- writes text
- publishes/schedules
- handles interactive elements

Creative:
- designs story graphics where design is needed
- edits story video where needed

## Story types

- repurpose feed post
- behind the scenes
- client proof
- testimonial
- poll
- question
- event reminder
- offer reminder
- FAQ
- link/CTA
- team moment
- quick tip

## Do not simply repost feed content every time

Stories should feel more immediate and conversational.

## Design

Keep:
- text large
- layout simple
- safe areas clear
- CTA obvious
- branding consistent

## Interactive elements

Use where relevant:
- poll
- questions
- link
- countdown
- slider

Do not use interaction stickers randomly.

## Approval

For standard pre-planned promotional stories:
follow the client's approval rules.

For low-risk reactive stories:
use the account-specific rules agreed with the client.

## Archive

Save significant story assets in the client Drive where useful.
