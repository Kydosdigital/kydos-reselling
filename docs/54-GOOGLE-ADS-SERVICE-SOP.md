# Google Ads Service SOP

This SOP teaches the agency owner how to sell, scope, hand off, quality-check and report Google Ads.

It is not a complete PPC specialist course.

## Kydos pricing

- £300/month management where monthly ad spend is up to £1,000
- 15% of monthly ad spend where spend exceeds £1,000

Ad spend is separate.

## Best fit

Google Search is particularly useful where:
- customers actively search for the service
- commercial intent exists
- geography can be defined
- landing page is strong
- conversion tracking can be configured

## Before selling

Check:
- search demand
- offer
- geography
- budget
- competition
- landing page
- tracking
- call/form handling

## Onboarding

Collect:
- business
- services
- locations
- target customers
- priority services
- monthly budget
- previous Google Ads history
- Google Ads access
- GA4
- Google Tag Manager
- Search Console where useful
- website/CMS access where needed
- conversion actions
- phone tracking setup if used
- compliance restrictions

## Campaign build

Specialist documents:
- campaign type
- service theme
- keyword themes
- match types
- negative keywords
- geography
- bidding strategy
- budget
- ad copy
- assets/extensions
- landing page
- conversion actions

## Keyword principle

Do not simply bid on every broad term in the industry.

Separate:
- high commercial intent
- informational terms
- competitor terms
- irrelevant terms

Use negative keywords.

## Landing page

The ad and landing page should match.

If the ad says:
"Manchester employment solicitor"

do not send the user to a generic homepage with no employment-law information.

## Launch QA

- correct Google Ads account
- correct billing
- correct geography
- correct budget
- correct keywords
- negative keywords
- final URLs
- conversion tracking
- call tracking if used
- ad assets
- spelling
- policy issues
- landing page mobile QA

## Optimisation

Review:
- spend
- clicks
- CTR
- CPC
- search terms
- conversions
- cost per conversion
- impression share where useful
- quality/relevance
- landing page
- lead quality
- sales outcome

## Search term review

This is essential.

Regularly:
- add negatives
- identify new profitable themes
- identify wasted spend

## Reporting

Monthly.

Explain:
- what money was spent on
- conversions
- quality
- waste reduced
- best campaigns
- issues
- next steps

## Agency owner rule

Do not sell Google Ads if the actual specialist cannot demonstrate competent search-term, tracking, conversion and optimisation discipline.
