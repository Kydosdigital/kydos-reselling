# Client Renewal SOP

## Kydos commercial model

A new client can use the first paid month to experience the service.

After that, continuation can move to:
- 3 months
- 6 months
- 12 months

The agency does not need to pressure the client before they have seen the service.

## Renewal timing

Start renewal discussion before the current commitment ends.

Suggested:

- 30 days before end date
- earlier for high-value/complex accounts

## Renewal review

Before contacting client, review:

- performance
- deliverables
- client feedback
- payment history
- open problems
- future goals
- appropriate package

## Renewal conversation

Structure:

1. recap period
2. what was delivered
3. what was learnt
4. what should happen next
5. confirm continuation/package
6. send updated agreement where required

## Do not renew blindly

A client may need:
- same package
- upgrade
- downgrade
- different service
- pause
- exit

Recommend based on need.

## CRM

Record:

- renewal date
- current term
- proposed term
- package
- decision
- signed agreement
- next billing date

## Cancellation

If client chooses to leave:

- follow agreement
- confirm final billing
- complete offboarding
- remove access
- transfer agreed client-owned assets
- request feedback
- leave professionally
