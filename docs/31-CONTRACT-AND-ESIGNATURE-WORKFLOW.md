# Contract and E-Signature Workflow

The agency should move away from relying only on emailing PDFs for manual signature.

The branded CRM should be configured to issue contracts electronically where available.

## Before sending

Confirm:
- correct legal client name
- correct package
- correct price
- VAT treatment
- start date
- contract length
- cancellation terms
- revision allowance
- service scope
- client responsibilities

## Workflow

1. Sales closes the package.
2. Correct service agreement/template selected.
3. Client details merged into document.
4. Sales or authorised manager checks all commercial fields.
5. Contract sent electronically.
6. Client signs.
7. Completed copy stored.
8. CRM records signature/completion.
9. Payment is confirmed.
10. Onboarding starts.

## Do not use one generic scope for everything

Use:
- Master Client Service Agreement
- service-specific schedule

Possible schedules:
- Social Media
- Meta Ads
- Google Ads
- SEO
- Website
- Landing Page
- CRM and Automation

## Key Kydos terms to reflect

Subject to final legal drafting:
- payment in advance
- first paid month before longer-term continuation
- 3, 6 or 12 month continuation options
- one month cancellation notice where applicable
- two revision rounds for standard content
- client approval responsibilities
- no guaranteed marketing result
- client must provide accurate information and access
- ad spend separate from management fee where applicable

## Storage

Store executed agreements in:

Client Drive / 01 Onboarding / Contracts

And retain a CRM record.

## Changes

Do not change pricing/scope only in WhatsApp.

Any material change should be recorded through:
- written variation
- updated schedule
- approved quotation
- new agreement where appropriate
