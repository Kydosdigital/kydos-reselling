# NatWest Business Banking Guide

Last reviewed: 31 August 2026

## Kydos recommendation

Kydos currently uses NatWest as its main business bank.

ANNA was used for the initial formation route, then Kydos moved its main banking to NatWest.

This is the recommended Kydos model, not a statement that every agency must use NatWest.

## Why NatWest fits the Kydos model

A key benefit is the FreeAgent relationship.

NatWest currently states that eligible business current account customers can access FreeAgent without a separate FreeAgent subscription charge while they retain the qualifying account. Optional add-ons can still cost extra.

FreeAgent can support:
- bank transaction feeds
- invoicing
- expenses
- cashflow visibility
- bookkeeping
- tax estimates
- accountant collaboration

## Setup sequence

### 1. Prepare the company

Before applying, have:
- Companies House company number
- company legal name
- business address
- director details
- ownership details
- identification
- expected turnover
- expected transaction activity
- website or business description where available

### 2. Apply for the appropriate business account

Use NatWest's current eligibility criteria and account-selection process.

Do not promise approval.

Bank onboarding is subject to eligibility, compliance and credit or risk checks.

### 3. Complete verification

Respond promptly to any request for:
- identification
- proof of address
- business activity
- source of funds
- expected transactions

### 4. Activate online and mobile banking

Once active:
- set secure credentials
- enable notifications
- store recovery details securely
- do not share banking login details with staff

### 5. Connect FreeAgent

Connect the qualifying NatWest business account to FreeAgent.

Invite the accountant using FreeAgent's accountant-access method rather than sharing the owner's credentials.

## Banking control rules

- Never mix routine personal spending with the company account
- Reconcile transactions
- Store invoices and receipts
- Use clear payment references
- Restrict staff payment permissions
- Review subscriptions monthly
- Keep a tax reserve
- Keep enough working capital to cover staff and software

## Sponsor-licence caution

Do not teach participants that NatWest itself is a Home Office requirement.

Where a sponsor-licence application requires banking evidence, the current Home Office guidance should be checked at that time.

Kydos recommends NatWest because it works well for Kydos's operating and accounting model.

## Backup banking

Kydos does not currently maintain a dedicated second backup business bank.

As the agency grows, the owner may consider operational resilience, but this is not a launch requirement in the Kydos blueprint.
