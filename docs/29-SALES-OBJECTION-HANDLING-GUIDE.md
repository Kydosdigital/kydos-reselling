# Sales Objection Handling Guide

The objective is to understand the objection, not overpower the prospect.

## "It's too expensive"

Response:

"I understand. Is it the overall budget that's the issue, or are you not yet sure the service is worth that amount?"

Then address the real issue.

Do not immediately discount.

## "I need to think about it"

Response:

"Of course. What part do you mainly want to think through, the price, the service itself, or whether now is the right time?"

Agree a follow-up date.

## "I need to speak to my partner/team"

Response:

"That makes sense. Is there anything you think they will want to understand before making the decision?"

Offer:
- a written summary
- a joint follow-up call
- agreed follow-up date

## "Can you guarantee results?"

Response:

"We can guarantee the work and process we've agreed to deliver, but we don't guarantee a specific number of sales or leads because results also depend on the offer, market, budget, website, sales follow-up and other factors."

## "Another agency is cheaper"

Response:

"They may be, and price is one factor. The more useful comparison is exactly what is being delivered, who is doing the work, how often, how communication works and what happens after a lead comes in."

Do not insult competitors.

## "Can I cancel anytime?"

Explain the actual agreement accurately.

Kydos model:
- first paid month allows the client to experience the service
- continuation can be on a 3, 6 or 12 month agreement
- cancellation terms must match the signed agreement

## "Can you do a free trial?"

Default:
No free delivery.

The first month is paid.

## "Can you reduce the price?"

Only discount if:
- authorised
- commercially sensible
- recorded
- scope is adjusted where necessary

Never create private pricing that operations does not know about.

## "Send me information"

Send it, but also agree the next step.

"When would be a sensible time for me to come back to you after you've had a look?"
