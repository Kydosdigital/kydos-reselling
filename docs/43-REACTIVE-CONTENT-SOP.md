# Reactive Content SOP

Reactive content responds to:

- trends
- news
- industry developments
- events
- client milestones
- unexpected opportunities

## Rule

A reactive post normally replaces one planned item.

It does not automatically increase the monthly content volume.

## Use reactive content when

- it is relevant to the client's audience
- the client has something useful to add
- timing matters
- the trend fits the brand
- the team can produce it quickly without dropping quality

## Do not use when

- the trend has nothing to do with the business
- it could create reputational risk
- facts are unclear
- the client is in a regulated sector and approval is needed
- the joke conflicts with brand tone
- the post would look forced

## Process

1. Account Manager identifies opportunity.
2. Check relevance.
3. Check facts.
4. Check Content Rules Sheet.
5. Decide which planned item will be replaced.
6. Draft concept/copy.
7. Get internal approval where needed.
8. Get client approval if required.
9. Creative produces fast-turnaround asset if needed.
10. Publish while still timely.

## Speed does not remove quality control

Even reactive content must be:
- accurate
- correctly branded
- correctly spelled
- appropriate
