# Kydos Source of Truth

This document records the current Kydos Digital operating rules that should be treated as the default until a later decision replaces them.

## Company

- Legal name: KYDOS DIGITAL LTD
- Core model: UK digital marketing agency
- Main acquisition channel: Meta Ads
- Programme model: teaches customers to build and own their own UK digital marketing agency, it is not a white-label or reseller programme

## Banking, accounting and administration

- Company formation route used by Kydos: ANNA Money
- Current main business bank: NatWest
- Accounting software: FreeAgent
- Accounting support: external accountant, paid monthly
- Client invoices: generated through the CRM
- Client payments: Stripe through the CRM
- Recurring monthly invoices: automated through the CRM
- Kydos is VAT registered
- ICO registration and data protection fee are maintained
- Professional indemnity insurance is believed to be active, the complete insurance schedule still needs verification

## Client commercial rules

- New clients may use the first month as a paid trial month
- After the first month, clients can continue on 3, 6 or 12 month agreements
- Monthly services are paid in advance
- Cancellation notice: one month
- Payment grace period: approximately two weeks
- Included revisions: two rounds
- Content is not auto-approved after the review window
- If approval is late, the team follows up and waits for the client

## Content deadlines

- Following month's content calendar due internally by the 25th
- Operations Manager or CEO reviews the calendar before it is sent to the client
- Client review target: 3 days
- Two revision rounds
- Weekly creative brief is prepared by the Account Manager after calendar approval
- Weekly creative batch deadline: Monday, 1:00 PM UK time
- Reactive/trending posts normally replace a planned post rather than increasing monthly volume
- Reporting is monthly
