# Business Insurance Checklist

This is an insurance discussion checklist, not a statement that every policy is legally required for every agency.

Kydos currently believes professional indemnity is part of its insurance cover. The complete current Kydos insurance schedule should be verified before the final worked example is published.

## Policies to discuss with a broker or insurer

### Professional Indemnity

Relevant because the agency gives professional marketing, advertising, SEO, CRM and website services.

Consider:
- professional mistakes
- negligent advice
- client financial-loss allegations
- errors in delivered work

### Public Liability

Consider if the agency:
- meets clients in person
- attends events
- works from client premises
- has visitors at its premises

### Employers' Liability

If the business becomes an employer, check whether employers' liability insurance is legally required for the specific employment structure.

Do not assume overseas contractors remove all UK obligations.

### Cyber Insurance

Consider because the agency handles:
- client account access
- CRM data
- leads
- website administration
- email
- cloud storage

### Equipment Cover

Consider for:
- laptops
- cameras
- phones
- production equipment

### Legal Expenses

May help with certain business disputes, subject to policy terms.

## Questions to ask before buying

- [ ] What is covered?
- [ ] What is excluded?
- [ ] Is subcontractor work covered?
- [ ] Are overseas contractors covered?
- [ ] Are paid advertising mistakes covered?
- [ ] Are website-development claims covered?
- [ ] Is data breach/cyber response covered?
- [ ] What is the policy excess?
- [ ] What is the indemnity limit?
- [ ] Does the insurer need to approve contract wording?
- [ ] Does the policy cover work performed before the policy start date?
- [ ] Does it cover clients outside the UK?

## File these documents

Insurance/
- policy schedule
- certificate
- wording
- renewal date
- broker details
- claims contact
- proof of payment

## Annual review

Review insurance whenever:
- revenue increases materially
- staff are hired
- services change
- larger clients are won
- the agency begins holding more personal data
- international clients are added
