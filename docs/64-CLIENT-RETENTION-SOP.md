# Client Retention SOP

Retention starts during delivery, not when the client says they are leaving.

## Retention drivers

Clients are more likely to stay when:

- expectations are clear
- communication is proactive
- work arrives on time
- corrections are remembered
- reporting makes sense
- strategy evolves
- the service connects to business goals
- problems are resolved quickly

## Monthly retention routine

Account Manager should review:

- client satisfaction
- response time
- approval behaviour
- deliverables
- performance
- complaints
- payment behaviour
- scope pressure
- upcoming goals

## Warning signs

- slower replies
- repeated dissatisfaction
- "we'll let you know"
- missing approvals
- refusal to book review calls
- payment delays
- questioning every deliverable
- asking for access/export of everything unexpectedly
- comparing competitors
- saying "we're reviewing costs"
- frequent staff changes on the client side

## Action

For an amber client:

1. identify issue
2. speak to client
3. agree corrective action
4. set timeline
5. follow up
6. document

For a red client:

Operations Manager or owner becomes involved.

## Never retain through pressure

Do not:
- hide cancellation terms
- make access difficult
- withhold client-owned assets improperly
- invent results
- promise impossible outcomes

## Retention through value

Useful retention actions:

- show what changed
- connect work to goals
- explain next month's strategy
- identify new opportunities
- solve recurring blockers
- improve weak parts of the funnel

## Retention record

Keep:
- client health score
- last review
- key concern
- action owner
- next follow-up
