# Immigration Adviser Referral Process

Kydos has an immigration solicitor/adviser referral route for programme participants who want advice about their personal immigration position or a future sponsor-licence application.

## Important boundary

Kydos does not assess visa eligibility.

Kydos does not guarantee:
- sponsor licence approval
- Certificate of Sponsorship
- visa approval
- visa switching
- settlement

The adviser provides the regulated immigration/legal advice.

## Trigger for referral

Refer when a participant asks:

- Can I switch from my current visa?
- Can my company sponsor me?
- Which occupation code should I use?
- Is this salary enough?
- Can I be the Authorising Officer?
- When should I apply for the sponsor licence?
- What documents do I need for my exact case?
- Will the Home Office approve this structure?
- Can my spouse/dependant do this?
- What immigration route should I use?

## Kydos can discuss

At a general business level:

- building a genuine company
- operational systems
- business banking
- accounting records
- payroll readiness
- HR systems
- staff structure
- client acquisition
- client contracts
- business records

## Referral process

1. Explain that the question requires regulated immigration advice.
2. Ask permission to introduce the participant.
3. Share minimum contact information needed.
4. Send introduction.
5. Participant agrees legal/advisory fees directly.
6. Record that referral was made.
7. Kydos only implements business-side actions after the participant has received appropriate advice.
