# CTA Library

Match the CTA to the stage of the buyer journey.

## Low-friction

- Save this for later.
- Share this with someone who needs it.
- Which one applies to you?
- What would you add?
- Follow for more [topic].

## Conversation

- Send us a message.
- DM us "START".
- Tell us what you're currently doing.
- Want a second opinion? Message us.

## Lead generation

- Book a discovery call.
- Request a quote.
- Enquire through the link.
- Complete the short form.
- Speak to our team.

## Website

- View the full service.
- See our packages.
- Explore the case study.
- Read the full guide.

## Offer

- Claim your place.
- Book before [real deadline].
- Enquire while availability remains.
- Start your first month.

## Local business

- Call us today.
- Visit us at [location].
- Book your appointment.
- Check availability.

## B2B

- Discuss your requirements.
- Request a consultation.
- Book a strategy call.
- Speak to our team about [specific service].

## CTA rules

Do not:
- use a hard sales CTA on every post
- invent urgency
- claim "limited spaces" if that is not true
- use multiple competing CTAs in one post

One clear next step is usually enough.
