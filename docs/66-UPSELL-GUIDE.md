# Upsell Guide

Upselling means moving a client to a more appropriate higher level of service.

Do not upsell simply because a more expensive package exists.

## Good reasons to upsell

- client wants more posting frequency
- client needs community management
- client wants reporting not currently included
- client needs paid acquisition
- client needs CRM follow-up
- client needs landing page support
- current scope no longer matches goals

## Example

A client on social management says:

"We are getting enquiries but nobody follows them up quickly."

Relevant solution:
CRM + lead automation.

Not relevant:
Sell SEO just because it is available.

## Upsell process

1. identify need
2. confirm evidence
3. explain gap
4. recommend package/service
5. explain price
6. update agreement/scope
7. update billing
8. update delivery team

## Avoid

- surprise charges
- unclear scope
- pressure
- selling services the agency cannot fulfil
