# Client Onboarding SOP

This SOP begins after the client has paid and signed the agreement.

## Objective

Move a new client from payment to a fully briefed, organised account with all information, assets, access and internal ownership in place before delivery begins.

## Kydos onboarding sequence

1. Payment received
2. Agreement signed
3. Onboarding email sent
4. Dedicated Google Drive folder created and shared
5. Google Form sent
6. Client completes onboarding form
7. Client uploads all required assets
8. Account Manager assigned
9. Creative assigned
10. Account Manager reviews the business
11. Research begins
12. First content calendar is prepared
13. Operations Manager or CEO reviews internally
14. Client reviews
15. Revisions are completed if needed
16. Client approves
17. Creative production begins

## Step 1, Confirm payment and agreement

Before onboarding starts, verify:

- [ ] payment received
- [ ] correct package selected
- [ ] VAT treatment correct
- [ ] agreement signed
- [ ] service start date recorded
- [ ] sales notes complete
- [ ] client contact details correct

## Step 2, Create the client Drive

Create the standard folder structure:

01 Onboarding
02 Brand Assets
03 Content Calendars
04 Designs
05 Videos
06 Approvals
07 Reports
08 Ads
09 Website and SEO
10 Account Access

Use the template in this repository.

## Step 3, Send onboarding email

Send the standard onboarding email.

It must include:

- welcome message
- Google Drive link
- Google Form link
- asset upload instructions
- access instructions
- expected first-delivery timeline
- who the client should contact

## Step 4, Client form and assets

The client should provide:

- business information
- target audience
- products/services
- goals
- brand assets
- competitors
- tone of voice
- social links
- important dates
- compliance restrictions
- content preferences
- approval contact
- access details through the approved secure method

## Step 5, Internal assignment

Assign:

- Account Manager
- Creative
- Operations Manager oversight

Record the assignments in the client tracker/CRM.

## Step 6, Account Manager research

The Account Manager reviews:

- website
- current social accounts
- recent content
- competitors
- audience
- offer
- priority services
- past client corrections
- prohibited wording
- brand rules

Create the Client Content Rules Sheet before the first calendar is finalised.

## Step 7, First calendar

The Account Manager creates the first monthly calendar.

Internal deadline for normal ongoing accounts:
The following month's calendar is due internally by the 25th.

For a brand-new client:
Use the agreed onboarding launch timeline.

## Step 8, Internal review

The Operations Manager or CEO checks:

- strategy
- relevance
- factual accuracy
- spelling
- brand fit
- content mix
- hooks
- CTA
- compliance
- repetition
- whether previous client instructions have been respected

## Step 9, Client review

Send to client.

Target review window:
3 days.

Kydos does not automatically treat silence as approval.

Follow up until approval is received.

Included revision rounds:
2.

## Step 10, Production handover

Once approved:

- Account Manager prepares weekly creative brief
- Creative receives only approved concepts
- deadlines are confirmed
- weekly batch is due Monday at 1:00 PM UK time

## Completion definition

Onboarding is complete when:

- [ ] agreement signed
- [ ] payment complete
- [ ] Google Drive active
- [ ] Google Form complete
- [ ] assets uploaded
- [ ] access complete
- [ ] Account Manager assigned
- [ ] Creative assigned
- [ ] Content Rules Sheet created
- [ ] first calendar approved
- [ ] first creative brief issued
